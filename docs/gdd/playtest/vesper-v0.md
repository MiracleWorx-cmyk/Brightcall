## Personal resource — Tithe cells

- Starts the match with **3 charged cells** (Tithe max for v0).  
- One cell seats in the shoulder mount (**active cell**). Two on the belt.  
- **Spend 1 cell** when you resolve a kit ability that says *Tithe* (see cards). Empty cells are discarded (trash).  
- **Reload (1/activation):** if the mount is empty and you have a belt cell, seat it for free at the start of your activation *or* as your once-per-activation reload — choose one rule at the table and stick to it for the session. Recommended: **free seat at start of activation if mount empty**.  
- When you have **0 cells left** (mount + belt), you are **Dry**: −1 Movement (min 2), and your Ultimate is offline.

## Fixed kit (one card per slot)

No draft among her card pool yet — v0 is a fixed tableau.

### Passive — Forge Manners

While you have an active cell: +1 Physical Shield.  
While **Dry**: lose that shield; the first time you take damage each activation, you may Move 1 toward the nearest enemy (Oru snap).

### Active 1 — Resonance Arc (*Tithe*)

**Cost:** 1 cell when you play this (if mount empty, you cannot).  
**Effect:** Move up to 1. Choose a 3-hex arc at range ≤ 2. Deal **Physical Power + 1** physical damage to each enemy in the arc.

**Level 2 (Ability Power ≥ 3):** that hit also deals +2 ability (scratch / chromatic) damage to one target in the arc.  
**Level 3 (Movement ≥ 4):** after the hit, Move 2.

*(Unlock levels with the shared unlock clock; gates still apply.)*

### Active 2 — Shoulder Seat

**No Tithe cost.**  
Move up to Movement. Then you may reload: discard an empty mount state and seat a belt cell if any remain. If you already had an active cell, you may instead **vent**: deal 1 ability damage to each adjacent enemy (white steam — flavor only; no Chroma).

### Ultimate — Reality Fracture (*Tithe*)

**Online:** round 4+ and not Dry.  
**Cost:** 1 cell.  
**Effect:** Move up to 2. Deal **Physical Power + Ability Power + 3** to one enemy at range ≤ 2. That hex becomes a **scratch residue** until your next activation: enemies who enter or start there take 1 ability damage. (v0: residue is **not** a full Seam curtain.)

## Traitor lean (optional reminder)

If you spend your last belt cell on Resonance Arc or Reality Fracture to delete an assassin, you are playing the emotional dump — legal, painful next turns.
