# Armor and exosuits

Visual and lore guidelines for Harvester hazard gear in Brightcall. English only. Nouns: [terminology](../terminology.md). Look: [art direction](art-direction.md). Measuring stick: [Vesper](characters/vesper.md).

These rules keep Brightforged and Chroma-fueled craft readable as **worn field tools**, not sealed mecha shells.

## Why they wear it

Exosuits and mechanical braces in Brightcall are **hazard gear**, not medieval plate or ballistic vests.

**Safe Chroma handling.** Bare skin on Tears burns or Blooms. **Brightforged** metal lets a Harvester grip and work Chroma without that raw cost. Without those metal surfaces — gauntlet, tool housing, brace face — a Harvester cannot hold weeping edges and survive the job.

**Power into motion.** The same frames conduct a crew’s chosen source into strength and weapon drive:

- **Kardia** — finite **Bright-batteries** and Brightforged charge (stable Core craft).
- **The Aureate** — raw **Chroma-fuel** through pressurized tanks, vents, and failsafes (volatile Resting Light heresy).

Cloth and leather still carry most of the silhouette. Metal exists where contact or power demands it.

## The cloth-first ratio

Agility keeps crews alive inside The Bright. Full-body shells slow a fighter and waste craft that is expensive and rare to forge.

**Read ratio for character plates and standees:** about **20–30%** armor / tech / mounts, and **70–80%** clothing, cloth, leather, and flesh.

Hazard frames sit **under** coats, harnesses, and field kit. Strategic cutouts, splits, and wear let mounts and braces read at a glance. The outfit is lived-in: ash, grit, and asymmetric damage from the job.

## Faction contrast

### Kardia — Brightforged

Refined. Elegant. Pearl white, faintly radiant. Compact braces, gauntlets, battery housings, and weapon fittings. Blue and silver hard-light accents on that pearl shell. Civic efficiency. Calm contained Bright light — never Tear neon as the fuel color.

### The Aureate — Chroma-fueled

Crude mass over stolen or hijacked frames. Heavy. Always running hot. Raw Chroma dumps through vents, valves, and failsafes so the line does not seize. Purple, white, and gold over industrial grit. Pressurized chromatic fuel tanks are the loud silhouette beat.

## Character blueprint: Vesper (House Kardia)

Reference for Vesper and other Kardia field fighters. Full kit: [vesper.md](characters/vesper.md).

**Coat over the exo.** Her tailored high-fashion trenchcoat wears **over** the mechanical braces. The coat is Resting Light civic-modern — not a closed cylinder that hides craft.

**Battery cutout.** A deliberate opening is cut from the coat shoulder so the round **Bright-battery** in the under-coat shoulder mount pushes through. Pearl spherical / Core-vault-adjacent cell with faint civic radiance and blue–silver hard-light — not Electric Cyan Tear spray as her fuel. The cutout exists so she can lock-and-click a belt sphere into the mount at combat speed without stripping the coat.

**Asymmetric Chroma wear.** The weapon-side sleeve is heavily torn, stained, and etched where Tears weep from the Resonance Glaive’s reality scratches. That side of the coat looks worked and rotten with Chroma contact; the other side stays cleaner civic cloth.

**Brightforged gauntlet.** Under the ruined sleeve, a refined mechanical safety gauntlet — elegant protection, not a monstrous claw. Brightforged metal so she can hold the Glaive and keep Chroma off bare skin.

**Silhouette order.** Resonance Glaive first. Shoulder Bright-battery (through the cutout) second. Exo braces third — visible at coat edges and splits, never the whole body.
