# Civic field kit and Brilliance

Visual and lore guidelines for Harvester kit in Brightcall. English only. Nouns: [terminology](../terminology.md). Look: [art direction](art-direction.md). Measuring stick: [Vesper](characters/vesper.md).

These rules keep Brilliance readable as **scarce civic craft under cloth**, not sealed mecha shells, hazard suits, or full-metal exosuits.

## Why they wear it

Harvesters enter The Bright in **civic-modern field kit**: coats, harnesses, leather, canvas. Sparse mechanical braces exist where contact or power demands metal — never as a sealed body shell.

**Safe Chroma handling.** Bare skin on Tears burns or Blooms. **Brilliance** metal lets a Harvester grip and work Chroma without that raw cost. Without those metal surfaces — gauntlet, tool housing, brace face — a Harvester cannot hold weeping edges and survive the job.

**Power into motion.** The same sparse frames conduct a crew’s chosen source into strength and weapon drive:

- **Kardia** — finite **Bright cells** (Brilliance shells holding bright-white Bright energy) and Brilliance charge.
- **The Aureate** — raw **Chroma-fuel** through pressurized tanks, vents, and failsafes (volatile Resting Light heresy), often on controlled-Bloom infantry given controlled Bloom.

Cloth and leather carry the silhouette. Metal appears only where contact or power demands it. Brilliance scarcity forbids full-body cast shells.

## The cloth-first ratio

Agility keeps crews alive inside The Bright. Fractured gravity and peristaltic maze motion reward parkour leaps between shifting blocks. Full-body shells slow a fighter and waste craft that is expensive and rare to forge.

**Cloth over cast Brilliance.** Brilliance is rare and expensive — carefully cast to fit — so plates stay cloth-first. **Kardia / Vesper measure:** about **one full arm and shoulder** of Brilliance, plus **one glove** on the other hand. Coat and cloth own the rest. Aureate frames run heavier on metal without becoming sealed hazard suits.

Braces sit **under** coats, harnesses, and field kit. Strategic cutouts, splits, and wear let mounts and braces read at a glance. The outfit is lived-in: ash, grit, and asymmetric damage from the job.

## Faction contrast

### Kardia — Brilliance

Refined. Elegant. Pearl white, faintly radiant Brilliance with **organic** cast curves and a very light blue gleam on the metal — solid-state resonant conductor (micro-fluting; no cables/hoses). Compact braces, weapon-hand sleeve fittings, off-hand glove, Bright cell housings. Blue and silver hard-light accents on that pearl shell. Bright cells glow with **bright white / off-white** charge (cyan is Chroma). Brilliance conducts Bright only in part — cubes stay easy to swap. **Bright streams** read as subtle white visual signatures of nearby charge (not a drain); enforced, they move civic power through the air; concentrated, they thin reality. Civic efficiency. **Zenith Relays** feed districts and can empower those air streams (appearance still open).

### The Aureate — Chroma-fueled

Crude mass over stolen or hijacked frames. Heavy. Always running hot. Raw Chroma dumps through vents, valves, and failsafes so the line does not seize. Purple, white, and gold over industrial grit — cast iron, rivets, rust, copper heat. Pressurized chromatic fuel tanks are the loud silhouette beat. Foot soldiers are controlled-Bloom infantry in robust, dangerous, hotter metal frames — kin to Kardia silhouettes, heavier on metal, still cloth-and-harness first. Baron leaders stay out of The Bright.

## Character blueprint: Vesper (Kardia)

Reference for Vesper and other Kardia field fighters. Full kit: [vesper.md](characters/vesper.md). Leading plate: `site/public/art/hero-harvester.png`.

**Coat over the braces.** Her tailored high-fashion **trenchcoat** — unique to Vesper among locked characters — wears **over** the mechanical braces. The coat is Resting Light civic-modern — not a closed cylinder that hides craft. Other Kardia field kit uses varied civic coats; do not clone her trench onto the roster.

**Bright cell cutout.** A deliberate opening is cut from the coat shoulder so the **cube Bright cell** in the weapon-side shoulder mount pushes through. Brilliance shell; bright-white Bright energy inside; seating shows the subtle white stream→wind of charge moving into the sleeve. The cutout exists so she can lock-and-click a Tithe belt cell into the mount at combat speed without stripping the coat.

**Weapon-arm Brilliance.** One full arm and its shoulder in organic cast pearl — metallic sleeve powered by the cube — the limb that feeds the Glaive. That is the body-armor budget.

**Off-hand glove.** One Brilliance glove on the other hand. Elegant. Not a second armored arm.

**Asymmetric Chroma wear.** The weapon-side sleeve is heavily torn, stained, and etched where Tears weep from the Resonance Glaive’s reality scratches. That side of the coat looks worked and rotten with Chroma contact; the other side stays cleaner civic cloth around the off-hand glove.

**Silhouette order.** Resonance Glaive first. Weapon-arm / shoulder Bright cell (through the cutout) second. Off-hand glove third.

**Oru read.** Very large. Black skin with faint stone glimmer. Short textured natural hair. Singular earring. Match the hero-harvester plate.
