# Armor and exosuits

Visual and lore guidelines for Harvester hazard gear in Brightcall. English only. Nouns: [terminology](../terminology.md). Look: [art direction](art-direction.md). Measuring stick: [Vesper](characters/vesper.md).

These rules keep Brilliance and Chroma-fueled craft readable as **worn field tools**, not sealed mecha shells.

## Why they wear it

Exosuits and mechanical braces in Brightcall are **hazard gear**, not medieval plate or ballistic vests.

**Safe Chroma handling.** Bare skin on Tears burns or Blooms. **Brilliance** metal lets a Harvester grip and work Chroma without that raw cost. Without those metal surfaces — gauntlet, tool housing, brace face — a Harvester cannot hold weeping edges and survive the job.

**Power into motion.** The same frames conduct a crew’s chosen source into strength and weapon drive:

- **Kardia** — finite **Bright cells** (Brilliance shells holding bright-white Bright energy) and Brilliance charge.
- **The Aureate** — raw **Chroma-fuel** through pressurized tanks, vents, and failsafes (volatile Resting Light heresy), often on bodies given controlled Bloom.

Cloth and leather still carry most of the silhouette. Metal exists where contact or power demands it.

## The cloth-first ratio

Agility keeps crews alive inside The Bright. Full-body shells slow a fighter and waste craft that is expensive and rare to forge.

**Read ratio for character plates and standees:** about **20–30%** armor / tech / mounts, and **70–80%** clothing, cloth, leather, and flesh.

Hazard frames sit **under** coats, harnesses, and field kit. Strategic cutouts, splits, and wear let mounts and braces read at a glance. The outfit is lived-in: ash, grit, and asymmetric damage from the job.

## Faction contrast

### Kardia — Brilliance

Refined. Elegant. Pearl white, faintly radiant Brilliance. Compact braces, gauntlets, Bright cell housings, and weapon fittings. Blue and silver hard-light accents on that pearl shell. Bright cells glow with **bright white** charge. Civic efficiency. **Zenith Relays** feed districts as energy substations (appearance still open).

### The Aureate — Chroma-fueled

Crude mass over stolen or hijacked frames. Heavy. Always running hot. Raw Chroma dumps through vents, valves, and failsafes so the line does not seize. Purple, white, and gold over industrial grit — cast iron, rivets, rust, copper heat. Pressurized chromatic fuel tanks are the loud silhouette beat. Foot soldiers carry controlled Bloom so they can survive the fuel. Baron leaders stay out of The Bright.

## Character blueprint: Vesper (Kardia)

Reference for Vesper and other Kardia field fighters. Full kit: [vesper.md](characters/vesper.md). Leading plate: `site/public/art/hero-harvester.png`.

**Coat over the exo.** Her tailored high-fashion trenchcoat wears **over** the mechanical braces. The coat is Resting Light civic-modern — not a closed cylinder that hides craft.

**Bright cell cutout.** A deliberate opening is cut from the coat shoulder so the round **Bright cell** in the under-coat shoulder mount pushes through. Brilliance shell; bright-white Bright energy inside. The cutout exists so she can lock-and-click a belt cell into the mount at combat speed without stripping the coat.

**Asymmetric Chroma wear.** The weapon-side sleeve is heavily torn, stained, and etched where Tears weep from the Resonance Glaive’s reality scratches (Seams almost opening). That side of the coat looks worked and rotten with Chroma contact; the other side stays cleaner civic cloth.

**Brilliance gauntlet.** Under the ruined sleeve, a refined mechanical safety gauntlet — elegant protection, not a monstrous claw. Brilliance metal so she can hold the Glaive and keep Chroma off bare skin.

**Silhouette order.** Resonance Glaive first. Shoulder Bright cell (through the cutout) second. Exo braces third — visible at coat edges and splits, never the whole body.

**Oru read.** Very large. Black skin with faint stone glimmer. Short textured natural hair. Singular earring. Match the hero-harvester plate.
