# Art direction

**Brightcall.** World: Aethora. Genre: Bright-punk Fantasy.

The look below is locked. The table still has to read at a glance. Words: [terminology](../terminology.md). Physics: [board glossary](../lore/pocket-and-core.md).

**Leading references** (locked visual truth for Seams and Chroma):

## Core aesthetic

**Locked.** Genre: **Bright-punk Fantasy**. Stylized realism, painterly finish, sharp brushstrokes, rich texture, light cartoon exaggeration for standee read. Beautiful and lethal. Arcane / Altered neighborhood as reference, made unique through Aethora.

**Locked.** The physical world is heavy: **Bone, Ash, and Greige**. Magic is toxic pressurized neon against that grit. Shadows are **bruised** (plum, olive, charcoal), never strip-book flat black. The Bright remains spectacular, not murky.

**Locked.** No grimdark mud as the whole mood. No soft organic landscape as the default ground. No blood, fleshy gore, or wound imagery in the Bright's visual language. No hyper-realistic 3D renders as the finish. The board is not a factory floor. The board is the chaotic, surreal interior of **The Bright**.

## Color system

### Physical world — Bone, Ash & Greige

| Name | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Alabaster Bone | Brutalist architecture, Kardia Brightforged ceramic accents and Oru skin read. Shows scratches and fight damage. |
| Heavy Ash | Weathered concrete, dust, raw stone. War-torn ruin weight. |
| Gritty Greige | Weathered leather, canvas, Kho'Lan cloth. Bridges light and dark without soft sand. |

### Shadows — Bruised Darkness

| Name | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Deep Plum | Deepest shadows, ravines, Umbra wells. Bruised contrast under neon. |
| Industrial Olive | Metal, copper, Aureate and Refract gear shadows. Toxic and old. |
| Charcoal | Explosion edges, soot around Seams, where light dies. |

### Volatile Chroma — toxic neon

| Name | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Pressurized Magenta | Flows and levitates on Seam glass. Oil-slick glow on bone stone. |
| Electric Cyan | Radioactive blue on reality tears and active Splinter tech. |
| Searing Gold | Liquid sun / hot plasma. Raw Call of the Light hunger. |

Chroma is not soft fairy magic. It is overpressurized chemical heat against a grey world.

### Interface — flat and clean

| Name | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Rich Cream | Text panels, ability cards, tableaus. Soft contrast under black type. |
| Structural Gold | Vector lines, icon frames, card edges. Premium read. |
| Chroma Gradients (cyan → magenta) | Energy meters, level strips, Splinter augments only — clean graphic of board chaos. |

**Contrast rule.** Costume and ground stay in Bone / Ash / Greige / faction accents. Tears on the board stay Magenta, Cyan, and Searing Gold. If they share one dominant color, standees disappear.

## The arena: inside the anomaly

**Locked.** Matches take place entirely inside The Bright.

From the outside, The Bright is a massive, slightly squished spherical phenomenon: a giant ball of glass-like light roughly a kilometer across.

From the inside it is vast, unpredictable, and temporally distorted.

### The Eclipse

**Locked.** The Eclipse is the outer boundary of The Bright. It expands as the Bright Core grows stronger and consumes more of Aethora.

### Umbras

**Locked.** Umbras are the darkest weak spots along the Eclipse. They are safe entry and deployment. On the table they mark where crews walk in and where Stitching returns a body. They are not cover zones painted across the board. Deep Plum shadows live here.

### Spliced terrain

**Locked.** The board is a chaotic patchwork of overwritten realities. The Bright consumes and alters its surroundings. A hex may be half the interior of a Victorian civic house, cut sharp against frozen tundra or ancient rock. Hex middles show that splice. Seams are curtain tokens on or over hexes — not printed borders on every hex edge.

Industrial forges, vents, and steel appear as **one terrain type** inside that splice, not as the arena's identity. Outside the Bright, Aureate districts lean darker industry under ivory towers. Kardia Brightforges work in bright, optimistic civic light. Inside the bowl, those atmospheres arrive only as torn pieces mashed into the anomaly.

### The Bright Core

**Locked.** The Core sits at the dead center of the anomaly. Ambient Bright energy across the bowl is generally survivable. Proximity to the Core is lethal. The closer a figure stands to the center, the more extreme the Bright exposure becomes.

### Lighting

Cinematic rim light separates standees from bone-and-ash ground. Ambient mood stays heavy in bruised shadows. Violent Tear-light on Seam curtains breaks that mood. Faction accents still win over Seam flood.

## Seams and Chroma

**Locked.** A Seam is not a crack in the dirt. It is not a laser beam. It is not a ground rift. It is not a hex border.

A Seam is a localized, paper-thin **satin curtain** of shattered glass that envelops an object, a body, or a piece of landscape. Looking through it is like peering through a fractured window draped over something. Everything trapped inside that curtain is rewritten while the Seam lasts — steel becomes clockwork and crystal; rock becomes alien ice; the alteration is heavy and complete across the enveloped volume.

**Cause.** Every Seam is Bright energy fracturing reality. Inside the Bright this happens constantly. Splinters are Bright-powered Null-Flint and are a primary way crews cast Seams in a match. Players can cast Seams. Event-deck Seam casting is not locked yet.

**Life.** Seams always weep. Magenta, Cyan, and Searing Gold **Chroma** (Bright-Tears) levitates and flows on the curtain — up, down, left, right — and oozes at the edges where the barrier meets the unaltered world. It does not spew from the earth as a fountain.

**End.** A Seam is fleeting, like Splinter power. When it ends, the original attributes return. There is no permanent scar token. Kho'Lan and Adumbra can cleanse / pinch Seams. Lumari may work the weeping Chroma.

**On the table (working).** An active Seam reads as an upright acrylic curtain token with a duration. When it ends, remove it. Touch / exposure effects live only while the Seam (or its immediate weep) is relevant to the card.

Street says Tears / Bright-Tears. Refract Labs coined Chroma. Same liquid. Only Magenta, Cyan, and Searing Gold — no White Tears color.

Reference: the knife half-normal, half-rewritten behind shattered satin glass; the box art Seam draping a monolithic ruin with Chroma weeping only at the boundary.

## Characters: acrylic standees

**Locked.** Characters are acrylic standees, not miniatures. Silhouette first. Slightly exaggerated proportions—larger weapons, obvious shoulders and cloaks—so the outline reads across a crowded fight inside the anomaly.

Detail inside the silhouette belongs on the card portrait. The standee carries outline, faction accent, and one prop that supports the loyal fantasy. One pose. Full body. Thick enough to cut from acrylic and still be recognized from the scrap.

The standee does not change when augments are tucked. Tucked strips on the tableau show what the character became this match.

The style guide calls these figures gladiators for silhouette brief only: fighters built to read across a table. Fiction remains people who walked an Umbra into a Bright to harvest, contain, pinch, or seize power. Courage under a hard job. Asymmetry and Splinter housing show the toll of the work. The art does not sell PTSD ruin as the default face of a crew. Kardia faces read open, proud, and almost static — performed composure — with feeling breaking through in posture and eyes, not in collapse.

**Locked.** The world is original. Silhouettes, symbols, and costumes do not quote League of Legends champions or Aristeia teams.

## Faction shape language and accents

Each faction has a distinct shape language and a dedicated accent that pops against Bone / Ash / Greige and Seam light. Two characters in one faction share the accent, never the exact same pose or silhouette.

| Faction | Accent | Shape language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kardia | Blue and silver hard-light on coats, braces, and tool fittings | Civic high fashion under ash — tailored coats, crests, etiquette held in the mud. Brightforged as braces, batteries, and weapons, never plate suits. Majestic containment units. Optimistic civic craft in the storm. |
| the Aureate | Purple, white, and gold on industrial mass | Cutthroat wealth. Stolen Kardia frames with hissing chromatic fuel tanks. Vents, canisters, polished display over grit. |
| Refract Labs | Green and orange heat glow | Unhinged experimental tech. Brass, glass, hazard gear, glowing cables, Splinter and Chroma hardware ready to fail loud. |
| Lumari | Cyan and magenta | Zealots of Bloom hunger. Tattered rags over bodies rewriting under Tears. Body parts may sit inside localized Seams. |
| Kho'Lan | White, red, and green on Gritty Greige | Earthbound. Ritual tools, Null-Flint, organic structured fabrics, stone or metal adornments. The catch in the name. |
| Adumbra | Black, purple, and orange | Sleek, sharp, calm. Intricate silhouettes that pass until detail betrays them. Disguised tech that suppresses and steers Chroma exposure. |

## Device types

**Locked.** Containment units and chromatic fuel tanks must read as different objects at a glance.

- **Containment units** (Kardia): beautiful, golden Core-vaults. Stable. Civic craftsmanship.
- **Chromatic fuel tanks** (the Aureate): erratic, pressurized Chroma canisters. Volatile. Cutthroat modification.

Proper model names remain open in [terminology](../terminology.md).

## What is already decided about the board and pieces

**Locked.** Hex board, two faces. Team games symmetrical. Free-for-all has its own layout. First playtest arena: the plain 9-by-13 board in the game-design doc. Painted spliced terrain comes after that test.

**Locked.** Ability levels are lines on a card. Augment strips stay readable when several hang off one ability. Physical power and ability power never share a color or a shape.

**Working.** Hex-edge arcane / physical border marks are retired with the old Seam-as-border fiction. New terrain read lives in hex middles, Seam tokens, and event cards.

## How the table reads

When someone looks across the table, information arrives in this order.

1. Which standee is which person, from silhouette and faction accent.
2. Which ability lines are unlocked (wooden cubes on the character card).
3. Which augment is attached to which ability, from the exposed edge alone.
4. Which global augments are changing the person.
5. What the current event wants, from one card left in public view.
6. Which Seam curtains are active, and how long they have left.
7. Device type: golden containment unit versus pressurized chromatic fuel tank.

Facing is not a rule. The standee should not imply that it is.

Anything that loses this order because the painting is more interesting than the icon gets redesigned.

## Cards, icons, and type

**Locked.** The illustration is painterly. The interface is flat, modern, and high-readability on Rich Cream with Structural Gold frames. Information over painterly detail on UI surfaces.

Icons are flat vector marks in the family of MOBA and ARAM skill icons, sitting on top of painterly art so they separate immediately. Threshold icons are identical across the whole game. Exact drawings are open. The two dials never share a color or a shape.

Type for rules and stats is clean, industrial, sans serif. Painterly lettering stays off the level lines.

Ability cards are a column of level lines. Cubes hide unopened lines. The card looks finished at level 1 and finished at full unlock.

Slot augments are strips tucked securely beneath ability cards. The only text that must survive tucking is the mechanical change on the exposed edge. Flavor on a tucked strip is wasted ink. Global augments are normal cards beside the tableau.

Statuses invented by one character live on that character's tableau in the same type size as a level line.

Deployment / Umbra zones are printed. Respawn uses them. The event is a card, not a board state players must remember.

## Production constraints

- Acrylic standees punish illustrations that only work as a full painting. Exaggerated silhouette does the recognition.
- Active Seam curtains may be upright acrylic with a visible duration.
- Tucked augment strips punish borders and frames that eat the one line of rules text.
- Cubes on cards need a margin so a wooden cube does not cover the next line.
- The team face of the board is symmetrical. The illustration accepts a mirror.
- Flavor can live on a single line under a character's name. Level lines stay clean.
- Colored rim light is part of the style. It cannot replace the faction accent.

## Out of bounds

- Hyperrealistic 3D renders as the finish.
- Seams drawn as ground cracks, lava rifts, laser walls, or permanent hex borders.
- Permanent Altered scars after a Seam ends.
- Chroma spewing from the earth as the default Seam read.
- White as a Tear color.
- A factory as the arena identity.
- Umbras painted as general cover instead of Eclipse entry and deployment.
- Miniatures as the default piece.
- Line-of-sight scenery.
- A unique frame per character that breaks the shared card grammar.
- Faction crests more recognizable than the silhouette.
- A second standee every time an augment is tucked.
- Any figure a player could name as an existing champion by outline alone.
- Blood or fleshy gore as Bright, Seam, or Bloom language.
- PTSD ruin as the default harvester face.
- Soft fairy-glow Chroma.
- The rejected graviton sentinel as a visual north star.

## The first art test

Paper prototypes stay flat color and icons. The first painted test is five pieces. A player who has not read the rules should be able to answer:

1. A Kardia standee and a Refract Labs standee. Which one is approachable civic craft (coat, crest, clean battery), and which one is the unstable lab?
2. An ability card at level 1 and the same card at level 4. What got added?
3. Three tucked strips on that card. What do they change, without lifting them?
4. An event card. What should you be doing this round?
5. A golden containment unit beside a pressurized chromatic fuel tank. Which faction owns which device fantasy?
6. An upright Seam curtain mid-duration. What is rewritten inside, and where does the Chroma weep?

Seam light reads Magenta, Cyan, or Searing Gold on shattered satin glass. If the painting wins and the token rule disappears, the Seam fails.
