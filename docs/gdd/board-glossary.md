# Board glossary

Table physics for Brightcall. Narrative truth lives in the [lorebook](../lore/README.md). Nouns live in [terminology](../terminology.md).

## What the board is

One match is one Bright, played from the inside — but the table shows a **patch** of the peristaltic brutalist maze, not the entire cube. Square concrete monoliths and **void gaps** (missing hexes) carve branches; living motion still guides organic matter toward the Core beyond the patch. Fractured gravity favors parkour leaps between shifting blocks. Torn Kardia and Aureate structures appear as poor reflections of outside reality. Floor hexes, voids, spliced paint, and Seam tokens share the arena. Umbras sit on dark approaches as deployment. Toward the Core-ward edge of the patch, light bleaches; toward the Eclipse-ward edge it darkens. Proximity to the true Core remains lethal off-map. Combat stress and organics escalate Seam violence in a zone. Playtest layout: [playtest/hex-patch-arena-v0.md](playtest/hex-patch-arena-v0.md).

Seams erupt as predatory nets — upright curtain tokens on or over hexes. Violent Chroma burst at birth; continuous weep while active. They are not printed hex borders. While a Seam lasts, whatever it wraps is rewritten. When the Seam ends, terrain and objects restore. A Harvester or weapon wrap is an Enhancement. Splinter-Crush casts synthetic Seams from crushing chambers. Residue may briefly block or confuse a route.

## Discharge

**Harvest:** secure a Bright Core with Null-Flint harvesting equipment / containment. The Core is taken intact instead of shattered into Splinters. That Bright ends in violent implosion. Survivors Stitch to their Eclipse crossing. A young Bright rises from a Seed elsewhere. Umbras are entry only.

**Destroy:** Null-Flint into the Core. Splinters. Implosion. Same collapse Stitching for survivors not erased by the strike.

**Pinch:** The Oru (and Penumbra cleanses) shut a Seam before its natural end. The pinch name is still open. No vault from that act.

Harvest or pinch Discharge pays victory points with kills on the same track.

## What the board sees

A zone or hill is often an active Seam curtain — fill and exposure work. An oddball is a containment unit in transit. Push and pull shove figures into or off a Seam. Kills bloom near live Seams or objectives. **Splintered** is the shared mutation offer from Splinter-Crush — draw three, keep one on those rounds — a synthetic Seam wrapping a weapon as Enhancement (Path A beside the tableau or Path B under a slot). **Stitching** drops you, returns you to Umbra, and keeps Splintered cards, levels, Growth Track progress, and points.

Round 15 finishes a build. The match ends at 15 victory points.

## Factions, one physics

**House Kardia** spend Brilliance, Bright cells, Zenith Relays, and containment. **Refract Labs** pioneered Splinter-Crush. **The Oru** wield raw Null-Flint only. **The Aureate** hijack containment, burn Chroma-fuel, deploy controlled-Bloom infantry. **Lumari** drink Tears. **Penumbra** leverage Bloomed cells and cleanse Seams.
