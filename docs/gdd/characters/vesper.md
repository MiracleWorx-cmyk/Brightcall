# Vesper

**Faction.** House Kardia.  
**Role on the roster.** First measuring-stick character. Mobile bruiser. Slow for a Kardian, durable and strong from Oru nature. Brilliance tech keeps her in the fight; it does not make her a specialist peak.

**One sentence.** Vesper walks an Umbra into The Bright because **Valen** — her dearest friend and former Brightsmith partner — was exiled after illicit Chroma work and now builds for the Aureate; she wants revenge for that betrayal. He designed and forged the Resonance Glaive she still carries.

**Leading plate.** [`site/public/art/hero-harvester.png`](../../../site/public/art/hero-harvester.png) is the visual north star for Vesper and Oru Harvesters.

## Who she is

She was a Brightsmith before she became a Harvester. In the forge she cast **Brilliance** — pearl metal fitted with care. She does not cast for the house anymore. The cells on her belt now are made by others and issued for the run. She still keeps Kardia manners: posture, etiquette, an open proud face that reads uplifting and almost static. The stoicism is performed. Oru bodies look carved and durable; inside they are fragile and loud with feeling. When emotion breaks the mask — anger, disgust, fear, joy — form slips, and she overcommits charge to close a gap and end the threat.

She is **Oru** by blood and **Kardia** by life — about **seven feet** of stone-glimmer stature in a city of smaller frames. There are not many Oru left in the cities, so she stands out on sight among pale Kardian crowds. She has lived in Kardia a long time, in the pearl-white civic spires. Walking Umbras as a Harvester sits in direct conflict with **the Oru** wardens’ anti-harvest ideology — that tension is real even without calling her a traitor.

When wardens come to the city borders to trade raw **Null-Flint**, many refuse her eye. Her cousin **Tarn**, a fierce Oru Warden, spits at her boots. To them she is not a traitor by title; she is a tragedy — an Oru in the white trench of the city that harvests the sacred Bright.

House Kardia was the Bright-order superpower before Aureate crime lords took control. Brightsmith pride remains. Ego bruises them even when they know ego should not lead. Vesper carries that pride: crest polished, coat civic, reload dignified — fake-stoic with underlying tension.

### Valen

**Valen** was her Brightsmith partner and closest friend — a Kardia prodigy. He ran illicit **Chroma-fuel** experiments beneath Kardia’s purity laws. A violent reaction destroyed his right arm and exposed the work. The house fired and **exiled** him. The Aureate funded a sleek Chroma prosthesis and made him their **Chroma-Smith** architect — harnesses for **controlled-Bloom infantry**, fuel lines, hijacks, and a brawler’s kit of prosthetic fist and Chroma-blade. Full brief: [valen.md](valen.md).

Before the exile, Valen designed and forged the **Resonance Glaive** for her — a masterpiece of Kardia engineering: an ultrasonic blade that cuts with a clean high-frequency hum. Every time she seats a fresh cell in the shoulder mount, she powers an exile’s genius. The weapon is her greatest asset and an open wound.

## Look

Gear rules that apply to every Kardia plate also live in [civic field kit and Brilliance](../armor-and-exosuits.md): rare cast-to-fit Brilliance under tailored clothing — for Vesper, about **one full arm and shoulder** of Brilliance, plus **one glove** on the other hand.

**Silhouette priority.** Resonance Glaive first (this is Vesper). Shoulder **Bright cell** second (this is Kardia). Mount braces third — visible through cutouts and coat edges, never the whole silhouette.

**Mood.** Lighter and prouder than grim survivor art. Bone, ash, and civic blue–silver under pearl Brilliance and bright-white cell charge. Not PTSD ruin. Not a mud-caked ghost. A builder who still believes mornings can return.

**Clothes.** A literal **trenchcoat** is correct: worn heavy-duty rain-canvas Kardia cut — high collar, storm flaps, off-white / faded blue, gold or silver trim — stained and worked. Resting Light **civic-modern** fashion under ash. Tailored fabric vest and shirt under the coat — cloth, not torso armor. Family crest on the lapel / chest, scratched and kept. Rugged canvas trousers; leather straps. Leather harness and tool belts for field work.

**Coat over the mount.** The trench wears **over** her mechanical braces. Keep the trench — do not close it into a solid cylinder that hides craft. A **deliberate cutout** is sliced from the weapon-side shoulder of the coat so the Bright cell mount pushes through for combat reloads. Wind-splits and worn openings elsewhere let brace ribs catch light. The coat frames Brilliance; it does not bury it.

**Brilliance amount.** The body-worn read is sparse on purpose. Pearl white, faintly radiant Brilliance covers **one full arm and its shoulder** — the weapon-side limb that feeds the Glaive, with the Bright cell mount seated there as a sleek Brilliance sleeve and shoulder socket. On the **other hand**, a single Brilliance **glove**. Coat, cloth, and Oru skin own the rest of the silhouette. Belt cells and glaive housing are small appliances, not more body armor. Not a sealed shell. Not cybernetic flesh-replace. Calm contained bright-white cell charge, not Tear neon as her fuel.

### Bright cell (must read)

**Eminence-pattern (VE-3)** Bright cells are perfect **cubes** — about the size of a Rubik’s cube (~7.5 cm / 3 in on a side), dense (~1 kg). Ultra-thin pearl **Brilliance** casing with circular cutouts that show the bright-white glass core and act as tactile finger-holds for a blind belt draw. Three mechanical connector pins seat into the shoulder receiver. Charged cells are **issued** for the run; empties are discarded. She does not forge today’s cells.

The **active** cell seats in the **weapon-side shoulder mount** and reads through the coat cutout as the second silhouette beat. Energy conduits run from the mount down the Brilliance arm toward the Resonance Glaive — cell → tool. When the cell seats or the glaive draws hard, venting valves hiss **pure bright-white** steam: clean, high-pressure, sharp, fleeting.

**The Tithe.** Under Aureate rationing, Harvesters operate on a meager allotment. Harvest is one of the most lucrative and important businesses on Oros, so the house still invests — Vesper is allotted enough charge for **two or three** cells per run. They would give her more if Kardia could afford it. She carries those few on heavy-duty belt sockets. She cannot waste a swing.

**Legacy cell.** She also wears an **empty old-model** Bright cell — a smith-era piece from her forge days — as a reminder of who she was. It does not power the glaive. It is memory on the kit.

### Weapon-arm Brilliance and off-hand glove

The **weapon-side arm and shoulder** are the Brilliance statement: cast-to-fit pearl from shoulder mount down the arm that holds the Glaive, Bright cell seated in that shoulder, conduits running toward the glaive housing. The trench cutout and Chroma-rotted sleeve frame that limb — they do not hide it.

On the **other hand**, one refined Brilliance **glove** — elegant protection for Chroma contact and civic grip. Not a monstrous claw. Not a second full arm of metal.

**Asymmetric Chroma wear.** The weapon-side sleeve is heavily torn, stained, and etched where Tears weep from the Resonance Glaive’s reality scratches. That sleeve looks worked and Chroma-rotted; the other side stays cleaner civic cloth.

**Tech ceiling.** No digital tech, screens, LEDs, internet, or modern firearms. Bright-punk power only. Vesper’s answer is the glaive and finite Bright cell charge. Lit cells, white steam vents, and conduit work on her kit are Brilliance / Bright energy craft, never Earth firearms or screens.

**Carry.** She walks into Umbras. The glaive rides a harness sling across the back or locks to a Brilliance hip/shoulder clip on the coat — pearl fittings that hold the housing without Earth magnets-as-sci-fi. Live Bright cells sit on the belt. The empty legacy cell travels with her. Tear jars and field tools hang where a mason would hang them. No vehicle fantasy on her default plate.

**Race.** Oru: very large stature (~7 ft), black skin with a faint stone glimmer — marbled stone read, durable like carved rock. Short textured natural hair. Singular round / geometric earring. Exterior reads mass and hardness. Interior does not. Match `hero-harvester.png`.

**Reload.** Dignified civic procedure made practical by the shoulder cutout and practiced across every harvest: hand to belt, deliberate push into the mount, heavy mechanical click, then the white-steam hiss. Forge-morning muscle memory under arena terror. Desperate slam-reload belongs to Splinter and Chroma-fuel fighters, not her.

**Moral meter.** A full bright-white charge holds her Kardian mask — calculated elegance. When the mount clicks empty and she is down to the last Tithe cell, polished manners burn off and raw Oru survival takes the body. Scarcity destroys her etiquette.

### Hero plate checklist (keep what works)

Keep: glaive mass and magenta scratch language on the blade edge only; trenchcoat civic cut worn over the mount; Oru mass, black glimmering stone-skin, short natural hair, singular earring; proud low-angle stoic posture; Fortiche / SD3 harvester lighting from `hero-harvester.png`.

Change: camera or coat so the **full weapon-arm and shoulder Brilliance** read, with the **cube Bright cell** through a clear **cutout**; show **white steam vents** on use; show the **off-hand Brilliance glove**; show belt cells and the **empty legacy cell**; glaive housing stays pearl (calm), charge stays bright white, Tears stay on the scratch/weep and the ruined cuff — do not paint her whole kit Tear-neon.

## The Resonance Glaive

A precision mason’s tool turned weapon: ultrasonic vibration wired to her Bright cell — Valen’s design. Brilliance metal through and through — readable as refined civic tech (clean housing, lit white charge), not a wooden fantasy polearm. It can cut stone, metal, and — at full voice — thin reality into a **scratch** (a Seam almost opening). Among blades on Oros it sits near the top of what refined Brilliance craft can do. It does not match a Splinter’s full rewrite, raw Chroma burn, or a Bright Core. That hunger is why two or three Tithe cells vanish in a single run.

**Safe Chroma contact.** Brilliance lets Vesper handle Chroma without bare-skin Bloom or burn. When Tears weep along the glaive, the craft takes the touch. That is why a Kardian Harvester can work Seam edges and scratches with a civic tool instead of dying on the first ooze.

**Reality scratches.** Side-effect of Bright vibration, not Chroma-fuel craft. Magenta/cyan Tears may weep where the cut almost opens a Seam. Kardia do not deal in Tears — this leak is frowned upon. Vesper finds the ooze disgusting. She still flirts with the edge it gives her against enemies — figurative and literal — because the glaive’s Brilliance keeps her safe enough to stay close. That tension is her moral line and her traitor lean.

On the board, scratches are not automatically a lasting Seam token. Working options: dropped Bright-Tear / Chroma residue, or extra chromatic damage on the swing. Rules not settled. A scratch can deepen into a Seam.

## Play fantasy

**Loyal.** Mobile bruiser. Hold pressure through durability and glaive reach. Manage the Tithe — two or three cells. Finite stability: safe tech, attrition weakness when dry.

**Traitor lean.** Emotion dumps charge into gap-close to delete assassins threatening her or her team. She still reads as Kardia when she does it — manners broken, crest still on the chest.

**Bad build.** All aggression, empty cells early, no plan when the mount goes quiet.

## Faction habit she proves

Kardia adapt. They do not own the “plant and hold the hill” fantasy — that belongs to the Aureate’s heaviest tanks. Vesper teaches adaptable Kardia play: move, brawl, reload with dignity, threaten space with a civic tool that should never have been a weapon.
