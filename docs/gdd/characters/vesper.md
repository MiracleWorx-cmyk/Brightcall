# Vesper

**Faction.** House Kardia.  
**Role on the roster.** First measuring-stick character. Mobile bruiser. Slow for a Kardian, durable and strong from Oru nature. Brilliance tech keeps her in the fight; it does not make her a specialist peak.

**One sentence.** Vesper walks an Umbra into The Bright because her dear friend and former Brightsmith colleague defected to forge for the Aureate — and she wants revenge for that betrayal. That friend designed the Resonance Glaive she carries.

**Leading plate.** [`site/public/art/hero-harvester.png`](../../../site/public/art/hero-harvester.png) is the visual north star for Vesper and Oru Harvesters.

## Who she is

She was a Brightsmith before she became a Harvester. She still keeps Kardia manners: posture, etiquette, an open proud face that reads uplifting and almost static. The stoicism is performed. Oru bodies look carved and durable; inside they are fragile and loud with feeling. When emotion breaks the mask — anger, disgust, fear, joy — form slips, and she overcommits charge to close a gap and end the threat.

She is **Oru** by blood and **Kardia** by life. There are not many Oru left in the cities, so she stands out on sight. She has lived in Kardia a long time. Walking Umbras as a Harvester sits in direct conflict with **the Oru** wardens’ anti-harvest ideology — that tension is real even without calling her a traitor.

House Kardia was the Bright-order superpower before Aureate crime lords took control. Brightsmith pride remains. Ego bruises them even when they know ego should not lead. Vesper carries that pride: crest polished, coat civic, reload dignified — fake-stoic with underlying tension.

## Look

Gear rules that apply to every Kardia plate also live in [civic field kit and Brilliance](../armor-and-exosuits.md): rare cast-to-fit Brilliance under tailored clothing — for Vesper, about **one full arm and shoulder** of Brilliance, plus **one glove** on the other hand.

**Silhouette priority.** Resonance Glaive first (this is Vesper). Shoulder **Bright cell** second (this is Kardia). Exo braces third — visible through cutouts and coat edges, never the whole silhouette.

**Mood.** Lighter and prouder than grim survivor art. Bone, ash, and civic blue–silver under pearl Brilliance and bright-white cell charge. Not PTSD ruin. Not a mud-caked ghost. A builder who still believes mornings can return.

**Clothes.** A literal **trenchcoat** is correct: tailored high-collared Kardia cut — off-white, faded blue, gold or silver trim — stained and worn. Resting Light **civic-modern** fashion under ash: Bright-punk grit with a modern silhouette read, not medieval costume and not Earth cyberpunk identity. Leather harness and tool belts for field work. Family crest on the chest, scratched and kept.

**Coat over the exo.** The trench wears **over** her mechanical braces. Keep the trench — do not close it into a solid cylinder that hides craft. A **deliberate cutout** is sliced from the weapon-side shoulder of the coat so the Bright cell mount pushes through for combat reloads. Wind-splits and worn openings elsewhere let brace ribs catch light. The coat frames Brilliance; it does not bury it.

**Brilliance amount.** The body-worn read is sparse on purpose. Pearl white, faintly radiant Brilliance covers **one full arm and its shoulder** — the weapon-side limb that feeds the Glaive, with the Bright cell mount seated there. On the **other hand**, a single Brilliance **glove** / gauntlet. Coat, cloth, and Oru skin own the rest of the silhouette. Belt cells and glaive housing are small appliances, not more body armor. Not a sealed shell. Not cybernetic flesh-replace. Calm contained bright-white cell charge, not Tear neon as her fuel.

### Bright cell (must read)

The **active** Bright cell is a Brilliance spherical / cylindrical pack seated in a **shoulder mount** on her weapon-side shoulder (or the shoulder that feeds the glaive). It reads through the coat cutout as the second silhouette beat. The shell is pearl Brilliance; the charge inside is **bright white** Bright energy. A short Brilliance tube or braided Bright-line runs from that mount toward the glaive housing so power is story-readable: cell → tool.

**Belt cells** are the reload kit: two or three matching Brilliance packs clipped with lock-and-click fittings. Same shape language as the shoulder cell, smaller or idle (dimmer). If the shoulder cell is hard to spot, the belt kit still teaches “finite Bright charge.”

### Weapon-arm Brilliance and off-hand glove

The **weapon-side arm and shoulder** are the Brilliance statement: cast-to-fit pearl from shoulder mount down the arm that holds the Glaive, Bright cell seated in that shoulder, Bright-line running toward the glaive housing. The trench cutout and Chroma-rotted sleeve frame that limb — they do not hide it.

On the **other hand**, one refined Brilliance **glove** — elegant protection for Chroma contact and civic grip. Not a monstrous claw. Not a second full arm of metal.

**Asymmetric Chroma wear.** The weapon-side sleeve is heavily torn, stained, and etched where Tears weep from the Resonance Glaive’s reality scratches. That sleeve looks worked and Chroma-rotted; the other side stays cleaner civic cloth.

**Tech ceiling.** No digital tech, screens, LEDs, internet, or modern firearms. Bright-punk power only. Vesper’s answer is the glaive and finite Bright cell charge. Lit cells and tube work on her kit are Brilliance / Bright energy craft, never Earth firearms or screens.

**Carry.** She walks into Umbras. The glaive rides a harness sling across the back or locks to a Brilliance hip/shoulder clip on the coat — pearl fittings that hold the housing without Earth magnets-as-sci-fi. Bright cells sit on the belt. Tear jars and field tools hang where a mason would hang them. No vehicle fantasy on her default plate.

**Race.** Oru: very large stature, black skin with a faint stone glimmer, durable like carved stone. Short textured natural hair. Singular round / geometric earring. Exterior reads mass and hardness. Interior does not. Match `hero-harvester.png`.

**Reload.** Dignified civic procedure made practical by the shoulder cutout: Bright cells on the belt swap into the exposed mount with lock-and-click pride at combat speed. Desperate slam-reload belongs to Splinter and Chroma-fuel fighters, not her.

### Hero plate checklist (keep what works)

Keep: glaive mass and magenta scratch language on the blade edge only; trenchcoat civic cut worn over exo; Oru mass, black glimmering stone-skin, short natural hair, singular earring; proud low-angle stoic posture; Fortiche / SD3 harvester lighting from `hero-harvester.png`.

Change: camera or coat so the **full weapon-arm and shoulder Brilliance** read, with the **Bright cell** through a clear **cutout**; show the **off-hand Brilliance glove**; show at least one **belt cell**; glaive housing stays pearl (calm), charge stays bright white, Tears stay on the scratch/weep and the ruined cuff — do not paint her whole kit Tear-neon.

## The Resonance Glaive

A precision mason’s tool turned weapon: ultrasonic vibration wired to her Bright cell. Brilliance metal through and through — readable as refined civic tech (clean housing, lit white charge), not a wooden fantasy polearm. It can cut stone, metal, and — at full voice — thin reality into a **scratch** (a Seam almost opening). Among blades on Oros it sits near the top of what refined Brilliance craft can do. It does not match a Splinter’s full rewrite, raw Chroma burn, or a Bright Core.

**Safe Chroma contact.** Brilliance lets Vesper handle Chroma without bare-skin Bloom or burn. When Tears weep along the glaive, the craft takes the touch. That is why a Kardian Harvester can work Seam edges and scratches with a civic tool instead of dying on the first ooze.

**Reality scratches.** Side-effect of Bright vibration, not Chroma-fuel craft. Magenta/cyan Tears may weep where the cut almost opens a Seam. Kardia do not deal in Tears — this leak is frowned upon. Vesper finds the ooze disgusting. She still flirts with the edge it gives her against enemies — figurative and literal — because the glaive’s Brilliance keeps her safe enough to stay close. That tension is her moral line and her traitor lean.

On the board, scratches are not automatically a lasting Seam token. Working options: dropped Bright-Tear / Chroma residue, or extra chromatic damage on the swing. Rules not settled. A scratch can deepen into a Seam.

## Play fantasy

**Loyal.** Mobile bruiser. Hold pressure through durability and glaive reach. Manage finite Bright cells. Finite stability: safe tech, attrition weakness when dry.

**Traitor lean.** Emotion dumps charge into gap-close to delete assassins threatening her or her team. She still reads as Kardia when she does it — manners broken, crest still on the chest.

**Bad build.** All aggression, empty cells early, no plan when the exo goes quiet.

## Faction habit she proves

Kardia adapt. They do not own the “plant and hold the hill” fantasy — that belongs to the Aureate’s heaviest tanks. Vesper teaches adaptable Kardia play: move, brawl, reload with dignity, threaten space with a civic tool that should never have been a weapon.
