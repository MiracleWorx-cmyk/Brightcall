# Aureate mercenary Chroma canister — design instructions

**Who.** Controlled-Bloom infantry (mercenary / foot soldier), not Valen, not barons.  
**What.** The soldier’s **chromatic fuel plant** — street mouths may say “vial”; the object reads as an **oversized pressurized canister**.  
**Fuel.** Same Chroma as the Architect. Exhaust law unchanged: about **60% Magenta**, **35% Cyan**, **15% Gold**.

Contrast north stars: Valen ampules = [`valen-chroma-arm-inspiration.jpg`](valen-chroma-arm-inspiration.jpg). Refract glass rack = `site/public/art/chroma-vials.png` (lab scale — not this).

**Object study (kept):** [`aureate-mercenary-canister-inspiration.jpg`](aureate-mercenary-canister-inspiration.jpg) — dark bolted frame, Magenta/Cyan/Gold Chroma in the cylinder, braided hose stubs, top vent weep. Product plate, not a full soldier mount yet.

## Job of the silhouette

One glance must answer: *this body carries the fuel; the weapon drinks through a hose.*  
The canister is loud. The soldier is robust. The hose is the threat line.

## Form

- **Scale.** Oversized relative to the torso — welding-cylinder / short keg / scuba-tank family. One primary tank, or a twin pair if the chest needs balance. Never Valen’s medical-ampule scale.
- **Body.** Thick-walled industrial metal: gunmetal, iron, bruised steel, purple-white faction paint scuffed on. **Well-made and robust** — machined, bolted, pressure-rated. Not rusted scrap fantasy. Not gold showcase. Not pearl **Brilliance**.
- **Mount.** Strapped to **back** or **chest** with heavy leather / web harness, or bolted to shoulder plates. The tank stays on the body when the weapon swings.
- **Face hardware.** Analog pressure gauges, relief valves, capped fill ports, warning stamps. One clear **sight glass** or slot where Magenta-heavy Chroma (with Cyan / Gold flecks) shows under pressure — dangerous liquid, not ambient neon paint.
- **Hose.** **Long** reinforced hose(s) from canister to weapon, gauntlet, or mount. Umbilical read: the tool is downstream. Couplings chunky; joints ready to weep. This is the opposite of Valen’s hose-free arm.
- **Vent / leak.** Magenta-heavy steam with Cyan and Gold at valves and hose joints — thick, lingering, staining. Louder and dirtier than Architect knuckle jets. Death-burst fantasy: if the soldier drops, lines can breach.

## Do

- Purple / white / gold as **faction marks** on straps, stamps, or painted bands — wealth of the house on cheap mass gear, not luxury plating.
- Controlled-Bloom body can look heat-tolerant and slightly wrong under the harness; the tank is still the fuel story.
- Keep cloth or partial plate on the soldier so the canister reads as strapped plant, not a sealed exo shell.

## Do not

- Micro glass ampules flush in a limb (that is Valen).
- Clean Refract lab vial racks or fountain-pen cartridges.
- Brilliance pearl, civic blue-silver hard-light, or bright-white Bright-cell cubes.
- External wires as decoration with no hose-to-weapon logic.
- Magenta-only exhaust; skip Cyan and Gold.
- Medieval rust bucket or fantasy potion bottle.
