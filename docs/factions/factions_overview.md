# Factions

Six names. Origins: [timeline](../lore/timeline.md). Feel: [lore factions](../lore/lore_factions.md). Board physics: [glossary](../lore/pocket-and-core.md). Words: [terminology](../terminology.md). Roster drafts: [faction design](faction-design.md).

## Kho'Lan

Say **KO-lan**, with a catch between the halves. Kho is the living world that does not weep. Lan is the promise to leave it whole. The Bright as divinity. Preserve balance. Pinch weeping Seams with ritual, Null-Flint, and other Aethorian materials. Oldest temper on Aethora.

At the table: silence, denial, empty Discharge. Earthbound. White, red, and green.

## Lumari

Bloom lineages from the First Survivor. Zealots who take Tears into the body. Faith as costume for power and Bloom hunger. Hunt a Vessel: a person who can absorb a Bright Core and live. Nobody has. Final title still open.

At the table: rewrite, health as fuel, fast adaptation. Rags as silhouette tradition. Cyan and magenta.

## Kardia

The civic heart. Larger nation. Working economy. Happier people. First Harvest. Alone build containment units and true Brightforged craft. House the Core. Refuse Chroma-fuel. Survive and prosper under Aureate theft.

At the table: adaptable. Finite stable Brightforged charge. Outmaneuver mass. Blue and silver hard-light. Not the plant-and-hold faction.

## Refract Labs

Split from Kardia after harvest science outgrew civic morals. Coined Chroma for Bright-Tears. Splinters, Bloom trials, Chroma-fuel. They do not work the Bright or Bright energy.

At the table: high peak, honest cost, Splintered tails. Green and orange heat. Speech shortens the firm to Refract.

## The Aureate

Kardia who left. Stole Cores successfully. Wealthiest power. Cannot forge containment. Keep hijacking Kardia units. Weaponize Chroma-fuel with defected smiths. Thin rich tip, poor laborer mass held by the dream of infinite wealth.

At the table: hold the objective. Biggest tanks. Late hijack, mass, death-burst fuel lines. Purple, white, and gold.

## Adumbra

Secret order. Suppress or cure Bloom. Cleanse Seams. Steer the Chroma-exposed. Pity the amplified.

At the table: push, pull, leverage, cleanse. Function alone. Black, purple, and orange.

## Order of design work

Kardia first (Vesper as measuring stick), then the Aureate as hold-objective contrast, then Refract Labs, Kho'Lan, Lumari, and Adumbra.
