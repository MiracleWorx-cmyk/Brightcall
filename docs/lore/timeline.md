# Timeline

Three ages on Aethora. Physics: [The Bright](pocket-and-core.md). Energies: [Chroma and Bright](lore_the_energy.md).

Hard light. Bright weather. A silent sphere. A culture that names its seasons after the Bright's mood.

## The First Bright

There was one Bright, and Aethora had always known it.

A hyper-saturated sphere hung in cut ground: silent, even, bright enough to read a name by. Seams erupted wherever The Bright cast its net — shattered satin curtains that enveloped and rewrote matter. Those Seams wept Tears at their edges. The Bright drank that liquid to feed itself. Near the Core, Bright energy poured out — heavier, rarer, available only to whoever reached the nucleus.

Culture grew around the sphere. Ordinary seasons did not matter. The Bright's temper did. When the seams started weeping, everyone understood. The omen was older than any faction.

The danger had a name: **Call of the Light**. A hypnotic song pulled living matter toward the Bright Core. Organic fuel calmed unrest after the sphere went hungry. Harmony meant respect. Harmony never meant safety.

### Kho'Lan and fanatics

The people of the First Bright treated the sphere as divinity. The Kho'Lan temper was already there: preserve the balance, do not meddle. Kho is the living world that does not weep. Lan is the promise to leave it whole. They pronounce a catch between the two. Inside that reverence lived fanatics who wanted more than prayer.

### The First Survivor

Someone came out.

Heavily Bloomed. Dying. Incredibly strong. Tears leaked from the alterations. Clothing would not fit the rewritten body, so the Survivor wore rags. Fanatics named the Survivor the human shape of the deity inside the Bright. They drank the leaked Tears as ritual. Most died. Some took small alterations and lived. Lineages that endured grew tolerant. Tolerance became hierarchy. Those fanatics organized into what the Resting Light still calls **Lumari**. Rags became tradition.

### The split toward the heart

The Survivor changed the argument. Organized investigation began. From Kho'Lan ground grew a second will: a perfect life, controlled, in sync with the Bright. That will becomes **Kardia**, the civic heart. Kho'Lan stay with the pause. The balance is holy. Meddling is the risk.

### The First Strike

Rath lost his wife and child to the Call of the Light.

He walked an Umbra into the Bright with Null-Flint and threw the crystal into the Bright Core. The flint did not bloom into fire. It imploded into vacuum. Bright energy and the sphere's charge poured into shattered shards. **Splinters** were born. The landmark went out. Rath died in the wreck.

When a Bright ends, a young Bright seeds elsewhere. Aethora entered the road.

## The Traveling Light

Peoples followed reseeding spheres.

Camps moved with each Glimmer. Too close meant the Call. Too far meant polar cold. The Bright still set Aethora's poles of radiance and frost. Fixed sites were not yet proven. This age is the long road between lights.

### The First Harvest

Kardia learned to contain a Bright Core.

They built a Null-Flint **containment unit**: hammer and ignition that implodes instead of explodes, a controlled vacuum. They reached the Core through its Seams, drew Bright energy into custody, and walked a living Core out. That act is the **First Harvest**. Brightforged civic power begins here.

The Traveling Light ends. Settled order becomes possible.

## The Resting Light

This is the present.

Fixed Bright sites are mapped. Cities rise on the safe ring outside the Eclipse. Mood language hardens into calendar: Glimmer / New-Light, Radiance / High Bright, Sature / harvest, Glare / the Blinding.

**Kardia** Brightforge Cores, forge containment units, and process Tears so mornings open. They alone hold the craft to contain a Core.

Researchers who want every use-case — stable Bloom, perfected Splinters, human trials, raw Chroma burned as fuel — break away as **Refract Labs**. They coin **Chroma** as the lab name for Bright-Tears. They work that liquid. They do not work the Bright or Bright energy. Kardia ban Chroma-fuel.

Kardia who crave more steal Bright Cores they cannot contain themselves. Theft succeeds again and again. **The Aureate** become a rival nation: vicious, cutthroat, short on knowledge, long on wealth. Defected Kardia smiths and inventors weaponize Chroma-fuel for them. Chromatic fuel tanks feed devastating, volatile arms.

The Aureate outgrow Kardia in riches. Kardia keep more people and happier working lives. Aureate society is a thin tip of the extremely rich over extremely poor laborers, held by the dream of infinite wealth. Kardia prosper under pressure. Aureate hijacks of nearly full containment units never stop.

Last into the walls come **Adumbra**: a secret order with eyes in every camp, resentful of what Chroma and the Bright have done to Aethora, holding a rare art that can suppress or cure Bloom and steer the Chroma-exposed.

Inside a Bright, crews still enter through Umbras along the Eclipse. They still say the old omen: *"The seams are weeping."* Death is Stitching back to the Umbra. Splintered gear stays. Timing worsens.
