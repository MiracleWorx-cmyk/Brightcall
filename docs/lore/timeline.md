# Timeline

Three ages of light on Aethora. The Bright’s physics live in [the board glossary](pocket-and-core.md). The two energies live in [Chroma and Bright](lore_the_energy.md).

Hard light. Bright weather. A silent Bright. A culture that names its seasons after the Bright’s mood.

## The First Light

There was one Bright, and Aethora had always known it.

A cube of hyper-saturated glass-like light stood in cut ground: silent, even, bright enough to read a name by. Seams erupted wherever The Bright cast its net — shattered satin curtains that enveloped and rewrote matter. Those Seams wept Tears at their edges. The Bright drank that liquid to feed itself. Near the Core, Bright energy poured out — heavier, rarer, available only to whoever reached the nucleus.

Culture grew around the Bright. Ordinary seasons did not matter. The Bright’s temper did. When the seams started weeping, everyone understood. The omen was older than any faction.

The danger had a name: **Call of the Bright**. It pulls living matter toward the Bright Core. How that pull manifests is unknown — nobody has heard it and lived to tell. Street mouths still say **the calling**. The Bright quiets when it receives organic material. When it hungers without that feed, Seams weep harder and unrest follows. Harvesters who enter become prey too — during consumption the Bright grows more violent, spends Bright energy as Seams that wrap living prey as Enhancements. Harmony meant restraint. Harmony never meant safety.

### Kho'Lan and fanatics

The people of the First Light treated the Bright as the axis of the world. The Kho'Lan temper was already there: preserve the balance, do not meddle. Kho is the living world that does not weep. Lan is the promise to leave it whole. They pronounce a catch between the two. Inside that conservation doctrine lived fanatics who wanted embodiment, not restraint.

### The First Survivor

Someone came out.

Heavily Bloomed. Dying. Incredibly strong. Tears leaked from the alterations. Clothing would not fit the rewritten body, so the Survivor wore rags. Fanatics named the Survivor the living proof that a body could hold the Bright’s rewrite. They drank the leaked Tears as lineage trials. Most died. Some took small alterations and lived. Lineages that endured grew tolerant. Tolerance became hierarchy. Those fanatics organized into what the Resting Light still calls **Lumari**. Rags became practical tradition for bodies that keep rewriting.

### The split toward the heart

The Survivor changed the argument. Organized investigation began. From Kho'Lan ground grew a second will: civic life controlled and powered by contained Bright energy. That will becomes **Kardia**, the civic heart. Kho'Lan stay with the pause. The balance is doctrine. Meddling is the risk.

### The First Strike

Rath lost his wife and child to the Call of the Bright.

He walked an Umbra into the Bright with Null-Flint and threw the crystal into the Bright Core. The flint did not bloom into fire. It imploded into vacuum. Bright energy and the Bright’s charge poured into shattered shards. **Splinters** were born. The landmark went out. Rath died in the wreck.

When a Bright ends, a young Bright seeds elsewhere. Aethora entered the road.

## The Traveling Light

Peoples followed reseeding Brights.

Camps moved with each Glimmer. Too close meant the calling. Too far meant polar cold. The Bright still set Aethora’s poles of radiance and frost. Fixed sites were not yet proven. This age is the long road between lights.

### The First Harvest

Kardia learned to contain a Bright Core.

They built Null-Flint **harvesting equipment** — a **containment unit**: hammer and ignition that implodes instead of explodes, a controlled vacuum that extracts a Core intact instead of shattering it into Splinters. They reached the Core through its Seams, drew it into custody, and that Bright ended. That act is the **First Harvest**. Brilliance civic power begins here.

The Traveling Light ends. Settled order becomes possible.

## The Resting Light

This is the present.

Fixed Bright sites are mapped. Cities rise on the safe ring outside the Eclipse. Mood language hardens into calendar: Glimmer / New-Light, Radiance / High Bright, Sature / harvest, Glare / the Blinding.

**House Kardia** Brightsmiths forge Brilliance and cyan Bright cells, raise Zenith Relays, and forge containment units. They alone hold the craft to contain a Core. Under Aureate oppression they keep the skill and lose the custody. They refuse Chroma-fuel and do not deal in Tears as a resource.

Researchers who want every use-case — stable Bloom, perfected Splinters, human trials, raw Chroma burned as fuel, synthetic Seam weaponry — break away as **Refract Labs**. They coin **Chroma** as the lab name for Bright-Tears. They pioneer **Splinter-Crush** crushing chambers and treat The Bright as a resource to exploit. They do not contain Cores, forge Brilliance, or run Zenith Relays. Kardia ban Chroma-fuel.

Kardia who crave more steal Bright Cores they cannot contain themselves. Theft succeeds again and again. **The Aureate** become a rival nation: vicious, cutthroat, short on knowledge, long on wealth. Defected Kardia smiths and inventors weaponize Chroma-fuel for them. Chromatic fuel tanks feed devastating, volatile arms. Baron leaders rarely enter The Bright. Foot soldiers take controlled Bloom so they can survive the fuel lines.

The Aureate outgrow Kardia in riches. Kardia keep more people and happier working lives. Aureate society is a thin tip of the extremely rich over extremely poor laborers, held by the dream of infinite wealth. Kardia prosper under pressure. Aureate hijacks of nearly full containment units never stop.

Last into the walls come **Penumbra**: shadow purists who treat The Bright and Chroma as alien invasion. They develop **The Voice** — acoustic or mental control of anyone who has Bloomed — and can also suppress or cure Bloom.

Inside a Bright, crews still enter through Umbras on Eclipse faces. They still say the old omen: *“The seams are weeping.”* Death is Stitching back to the Umbra. Splintered gear stays. Timing worsens. The calling still pulls.
