# II. The Bright

The Bright is the central anomaly of Oros — a vast **cube** of hyper-saturated light. Exact edge length is still open. Silent when it looks balanced. Blinding when you look straight into a face. It feeds on Chroma that weeps from Seams. It does not eject Chroma. It hungers for living matter. That hunger is **Call of the Bright**, **the calling**, and the layered meaning behind the game title **Brightcall** — one pull named three ways. The Bright’s known physical expression of that pull is the interior itself: peristaltic maze motion and fractured gravity that guide organic matter toward the Core. How the call sounds or feels to a living mind is still unknown. Nobody has heard it and lived to tell.

On Oros more than one cube can stand at once. Each match of Brightcall is fought inside one. Faith may insist every rising is the same Bright reincarnated. Science counts instances and tracks the loop: when a Bright becomes unsustainable and dies — Core harvested or Null-Flint-destroyed — the anomaly must exist and grow elsewhere. That relocation is how The Bright stays in harmony with Oros. The places where it can rise are **Seeds**. Resting Light civilization mapped those Seeds and built cities at their intersections.

The Bright quiets when it receives organic material. That appetite does not spare Harvesters. When crews walk in, the cube tries to consume them. During consumption it grows more violent. The Bright energy spent on the attempt erupts as **Seams** — paper-thin transparent satin sheets with fractured-glass texture — that wrap living prey. A wrap that does not finish the kill is an **Enhancement**: temporary rewrite of body or weapon. Full consume is death. Crews also cast **synthetic** Seams by crushing **Splinters** — the same satin web, fabricated on purpose, often wrapping a weapon.

Factions do not agree what this place is. The Oru read a living predator digesting prey. Lumari read a god’s deliberate will — and often one eternal Bright wearing new ground. House Kardia, the Aureate, and Refract Labs speak of industrial reality under Bright-energy pressure — their quantum model is still unfinished, but the Seed pattern is settled enough to build on. Refract treats the cube as a volatile resource. Penumbra reads invasive corruption. The lore does not force one creed. The cube remains.

Matches of Brightcall happen inside one Bright at a time.

## Faces and doorways

The outer boundary is the **Eclipse** — the six faces of the cube. It expands as the Core grows.

From outside, a face is bright. You can look through into a fractured, Seam-thick interior, hypnotic and lethal, like peering into a terrarium of light.

From inside, that same face is a dark, smoky barrier. Oros disappears. Home is gone from sight.

**Umbras** are the darkest weak spots on a face — dark doorways cut into a wall of light. That is entry. That is also where a body returns when **Stitching** catches a match-death. An Umbra is not an exit. You do not walk back out while the Bright still stands. You do not leave through a bright face. The only endings are rare **Null-Flint** acts against the Core:

- **Destroy** — Null-Flint into the Core. Vacuum. The Core shatters into Splinters. Violent implosion. The Bright is gone.
- **Harvest** — Null-Flint harvesting equipment extracts the Core intact into a **containment unit**. Violent implosion still follows. The Bright is gone.

Survivors of that collapse who were not erased by the Null-Flint strike itself are Stitched back to when they crossed the Eclipse. The Bright that just ended does not stay gone. Across Oros a young cube rises from a Seed. Someone else’s New-Light begins. The harvest at one site feeds the calendar of the next.

## Living terrain

Inside, the Bright is very bright. Brighter ground means more potent Bright energy. Brightness climbs toward the Core.

This is not an empty field of glow. Inside the cube a **monolithic living terrain** rises — towering gates, fused walls, square concrete masses, Bone and Ash stone warped by Seam light. The Bright’s defense fills the volume: an immune system that protects the Core and cages whoever the calling lured in. Claustrophobic. Brutalist. The maze moves in **peristaltic waves** — gut-like contractions that grind colossal blocks and passages in a harsh liminal geometry, always trying to guide organic matter closer to the Core.

**Fractured gravity** sits in pockets across that maze. Local pulls and shears favor extreme mobility: Harvesters leap, vault, and parkour between shifting masses. Cloth-first civic kit stays light enough to use that physics. Sealed full-metal shells and hazard-suit mechs do not belong here — Brilliance is scarce, and coats and harnesses own the silhouette.

Torn **Kardia** and **Aureate** structures appear mashed into the monoliths — civic towers, forges, industrial ribs — a poor attempt by the Bright to resemble reality outside. Reflections of Oros, wrong and incomplete, fused into the cage.

Seams erupt constantly. At the exact moment of eruption, Magenta, Cyan, and Searing Gold Chroma burst from the cracks and linger in the air. While the Seam lasts, those Tears also weep together through the fractures and along the edges. The sheet itself is clear shattered satin — never a monochrome colored wall. When a Seam on terrain or an object ends, the original returns. Brief unstable residue may linger and confuse a route. Residue is afterglow, not a permanent scar. Combat stress and organic presence escalate Seam violence in a zone — the Bright’s immune answer to prey that fights.

External contact with Chroma or a Seam burns and damages. **Bloom** — permanent severe mutation — happens only when Chroma enters the body internally: eaten, drunk, or injected. Enhancement is the temporary wrap. Bloom is the lasting rewrite of flesh.

## Two energies

**Chroma** is torn reality made liquid. Street says Tears or Bright-Tears. Refract Labs coined Chroma and the lab word spread. It can be harvested wherever Seams erupt and weep. Outside a Bright, Bright energy drawn from a contained Core can force new Seams so Chroma farms far from the cube. The leak becomes portable. The appetite follows.

**Bright energy** is what the Bright Core emits at close range. It is also the force that causes every Seam — appetite nets inside the anomaly, and synthetic nets wherever a Splinter is crushed. Inside the cube the ambient light is generally survivable. Proximity to the Core is lethal. Bright energy reads as bright white. Refined **Bright cells** hold that same white charge in Brilliance shells. Cyan belongs to Chroma, not to cells.

The **Bright Core** sits at the center as a perfectly smooth, blindingly white **sphere** of liquid light — a reverse black hole of light. Square maze geometry yields to that round nucleus. It emits enormous Bright energy. It does not bleed Chroma. Seams crowd it and weep. What the **inside** of a Core looks like is unknown. **Harvest** means taking that Core. Factions want it for different ends. Pure exposure near the nucleus is fleeting temporal rewrite at lethal scale. The only way to hold that power and live is to absorb a Core into a living **Vessel**. Nobody has. Lumari still hunt that person.

## How the Resting Light spends the leak

**Bloom** — Tears taken into the body. Lumari drink toward excess. The Aureate inject foot soldiers for controlled Bloom so those bodies can survive crude Chroma-fuel heat.

**Enhancement** — a Seam wrapping a Harvester or a weapon. Appetite or Splinter-Crush synthetic.

**Splinters** — Bright-powered Null-Flint shards born when Null-Flint violently meets a Core. Crush one inside a **Splinter-Crush** chamber and a synthetic Seam bursts out around the weapon. Refract Labs pioneered that armory. A body or kit built for shatter lines is **Splintered**.

**Brilliance** — House Kardia metalcraft: rare pearl-white metal cast to fit, faintly radiant, able to handle Chroma safely. Bright cells and **Zenith Relays** ride that craft. Containment units are Kardia Null-Flint hardware: pearl frames with golden Core-vault hearts.

**Chroma-fuel** — raw Chroma burned as fuel. Refract imagined it. Kardia ban it. The Aureate weaponize it.

**Null-Flint** — Orosian vacuum crystal. Hammer and ignition that implodes. The Oru wield raw crystals as their accepted craft. Kardia bind the same crystal into harvesting equipment.

When the seams weep, the old omen still holds. Chroma is in the air. The calling thickens. Harvest weather. The Blinding may be near.
