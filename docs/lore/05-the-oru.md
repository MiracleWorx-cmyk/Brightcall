# V. The Oru

Indigenous Wardens. The working faction name for the Oru who refused to industrialize the Bright.

Race and faction share the name. Not every Oru walks with the wardens. City Oru live elsewhere — in Kardia coats, in Aureate shadow, in streets that never learned the pause. An Oru body in an industrial city stands out: large stature, black skin with a faint stone glimmer, durable as carved stone. Vesper is one such body. Harvester work conflicts with warden ideology without needing the word traitor.

The wardens are protectors of natural harmony. Anti-harvest. They lived with the anomaly until the First Strike tore the old balance. Their opposite is Penumbra. Where Penumbra calls the Bright unnatural, the Oru call it part of the world’s food chain.

**In their telling.** The Bright is a living apex predator that shapes seasons. Seams are digestive tendrils. Death inside is digestion. Leave it alone to feed. Stop the harvest.

**Technology.** Null-Flint only. They reject Bright cells, Chroma-fuel, and Splinters. Raw crystals for vacuum combat and Seam pinch. Field cloth and Orosian materials. Null-Flint is rare.

**Want:** harvest denied; harmony kept.  
**Fear:** permanent industrial Core custody.

**At the table:** short Silence, denial, empty Discharge, Null-Flint pressure. White, red, and green.

**Roster (working)**

1. **The Seam-Sealer** — Force a weeping Seam shut. Deny Chroma. Score through suppression.
2. **The Null-Ward** — Raw Null-Flint vacuum tools. Short Silence on Splintered or Brilliance gear.
