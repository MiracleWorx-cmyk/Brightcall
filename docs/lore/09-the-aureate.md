# IX. The Aureate

Current powerholders of the Resting Light. Former Kardia who unified under their own flag and took the Bright order by syndicate methods — bribery, theft, extortion, hijacked containment. Wealthy barons and crime lords. Opportunists of the volatile harvest. Short on innate Brightsmith knowledge; long on stolen Cores and leverage.

**Tech base.** **Chroma-fueled.** Defectors and exiles — **Chroma-Smiths** among them — weaponized raw Chroma burn — a path Kardia refused. Robust, dangerous frames and armor: same civic-military family as Kardia silhouettes, but heavier on metal for the line troops, always running hot. Architects like **Valen** may wear sleeker personal craft. Exhaust vents thick toxic steam: about **60% Magenta**, **35% Cyan**, **15% Gold**. In Kardia that exhaust carries social stigma — dirty, unnatural, a hazard to bystanders.

**Foot soldiers.** Barons stay safe. Controlled-Bloom infantry — injected with Chroma so Bloomed bodies can wield those fuel lines and frames. Industrial capped Bloom, not Lumari excess. Penumbra can work those Bloomed soldiers from the shadows.

**Want:** unchallenged supremacy; hoard Cores; own the Chroma market.  
**Fear:** fuel lines tearing their own gear apart, or Kardia reclaiming Core custody.

**At the table:** hold the objective. Biggest tanks. Late hijack, mass, Chroma-fuel overclock, death-burst fuel lines. Purple, white, and gold. Pressure management: vents, valves, failsafes.

**Roster (working)**

1. **The Dreadnought** — Stolen mass running chromatic fuel on a controlled-Bloom body. The faction’s hill-holder. Immovable on a weeping Seam. Area pressure. If dropped, fuel lines breach and nuke nearby hexes.
2. **Valen (Chroma-Smith / Kingpin Architect)** — Vesper’s former partner. Exiled after a Chroma accident took his right arm; Aureate-funded prosthesis and unlimited Chroma resources. Designs infantry harnesses; brawls with sleek Chroma right arm and left-hand Chroma-blade; vents Magenta-heavy steam. Brief: [valen.md](../gdd/characters/valen.md).
