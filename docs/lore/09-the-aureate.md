# IX. The Aureate

Current powerholders of the Resting Light. Former Kardia who unified under their own flag and took the Bright order by syndicate methods — bribery, theft, extortion, hijacked containment. Wealthy barons and crime lords. Opportunists of the volatile harvest. Short on innate Brightsmith knowledge; long on stolen Cores and leverage.

**Tech base.** **Chroma-fueled.** Defectors and exiles — **Chroma-Smiths** among them — weaponized raw Chroma burn — a path Kardia refused. Robust, dangerous frames and armor: same civic-military family as Kardia silhouettes, but heavier on metal for the line troops, always running hot. **Valen** and other elite Architects wear sleeker personal craft — smith skill without Brilliance access. Exhaust vents thick toxic steam: about **60% Magenta**, **35% Cyan**, **15% Gold**. In Kardia that exhaust carries social stigma — dirty, unnatural, a hazard to bystanders.

**Foot soldiers.** Barons stay safe. Controlled-Bloom infantry — injected with Chroma so Bloomed bodies can wield those fuel lines and frames. Industrial capped Bloom, not Lumari excess. Their frames stay **well-made and robust** — not rust junk, not gold, not Brilliance — heavier metal running hot. **Oversized Chroma canisters** ride back or chest; **long hoses** run from those tanks to weapons and mounts so the soldier carries the fuel plant while the tool drinks at hose-end. Leak and vent on the same 60 / 35 / 15 law as the Architect’s refined arm. Design brief: [aureate-mercenary-canister-design.md](../gdd/references/aureate-mercenary-canister-design.md). Penumbra can work those Bloomed soldiers from the shadows.

**Want:** unchallenged supremacy; hoard Cores; own the Chroma market.  
**Fear:** fuel lines tearing their own gear apart, or Kardia reclaiming Core custody.

**At the table:** hold the objective. Biggest tanks. Late hijack, mass, Chroma-fuel overclock, death-burst fuel lines. Purple, white, and gold. Pressure management: vents, valves, failsafes.

**Roster (working)**

1. **The Dreadnought** — Stolen mass running chromatic fuel on a controlled-Bloom body. The faction’s hill-holder. Immovable on a weeping Seam. Area pressure. If dropped, fuel lines breach and nuke nearby hexes.
2. **Valen (Chroma-Smith / Kingpin Architect)** — Human. Vesper’s former partner; refined Kardia jewel-smith of Brilliance before exile. Ambition brought him to the Aureate; heart still with Kardia. Wealth worn as penance. Calculated strategist — slight forward lean; does not strike first; fights on break-points. Aureate-funded horological Chroma prosthesis (no Brilliance); temple Resonance frequency receiver; white high-collar formal coat-vest — never a trench. Designs infantry harnesses and canister/hose lines; flush forearm ampules; left-hand Resonance-inspired Chroma dagger; regulated 60 / 35 / 15 vents. Brief: [valen.md](../gdd/characters/valen.md).
