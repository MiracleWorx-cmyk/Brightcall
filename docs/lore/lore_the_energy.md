# Chroma and Bright

Two energies. Four ways to spend them.

Aethora tears and shines. The Bright is the cause. What weeps from torn reality is not the same as what pours from the Core.

Physics live in [The Bright — board glossary](pocket-and-core.md). Eras live in the [timeline](timeline.md).

## Chroma

Chroma is torn reality made liquid. **Bright energy** exerts massive pressure on a **third quantum field**, fracturing reality into **Seams** — razor-thin satin sheets with fractured-glass texture. At the moment a Seam erupts, Chroma bursts violently from the cracks and lingers at the origin. While the Seam lasts, Magenta, Cyan, and Searing Gold also **weep** together through the cracks and edges. That liquid is what street mouths call **Bright-Tears** or **Tears**.

**Refract Labs** coined **Chroma** for that liquid. The lab word spread. Refract Labs work Chroma. They do not contain Cores or forge Brilliance.

The Bright consumes Chroma to feed itself. It does not eject Chroma. More Chroma into the Core makes the Bright larger, stronger, and harder to predict.

Chroma can be harvested wherever Seams erupt and weep. Outside a Bright, Bright energy drawn from a contained Core can force new Seams so Chroma can be farmed far from the anomaly. The leak is portable. The appetite follows.

**External** contact with Chroma is pure hazard — pain and damage. It does not Bloom. On the board the Tear colors are only **Pressurized Magenta**, **Electric Cyan**, and **Searing Gold**.

## Bright energy

Bright energy is what the Bright Core emits at close range. It is also the force that causes Seams — inside the Bright constantly, and wherever Bright-powered tools (especially **Splinters**) cast a net.

Inside the Bright it is very bright. Brighter ground means more potent Bright energy. Proximity to the Core is lethal. For now Bright energy reads as **bright white** light; finer hue language is still open.

Extracted under Null-Flint vacuum into a Kardia **containment unit**, and held so the charge does not dump into Splinters, that nucleus powers **Brilliance** craft: city light, civic power, **Zenith Relays**, stable weapons — the First Harvest’s gift and curse.

## Applications

### Bloom

A **Bloom** is a permanent severe biological mutation. It happens only when Chroma enters the body **internally** — eaten, drunk, or injected. External skin contact does not Bloom. A Failed Bloom is the street story for an internal bind that killed. A Bloomed host carries altered light in the tissue and gains tolerance. The Bloom lasts while the host lives.

**Lumari** live on Bloom. They call the liquid Tears and drink it toward excess: extreme mutation for assassination and suicide missions. Lineages tempered by internal exposure climb their hierarchy by how much they can take without dying. They read The Bright as a conscious divine entity.

**The Aureate** inject foot soldiers with raw Chroma for a controlled Bloom so those bodies can withstand crude Chroma-fuel exo heat and pain. Measured. Industrial. Distinct from Lumari excess.

### Enhancement

When a Seam wraps a Harvester or a weapon, the temporary rewrite is an **Enhancement** — new combat power, changed fighting style or armament. Two sources:

- **Appetite.** The Bright’s consumption spends Bright energy as Seams on living prey over time. Full consume kills.
- **Synthetic.** A crushed Splinter releases dormant Bright energy as a localized fabricated Seam that envelops a weapon (or shatter-line on a body).

On the table, appetite wraps draft as Enhancements; Splinter-Crush wraps live on the Splintered path. Separate from Bloom.

### Splinters and Splinter-Crush

**Splinters** are Bright-powered Null-Flint shards. They form when Null-Flint violently meets a Bright Core — smashed against it in harvest, or born when a Core shatters under Null-Flint (First Strike / destroy). Dormant, highly unstable anomaly energy waits inside.

Crush or destroy a Splinter on purpose and it triggers a **synthetic Seam**: the same glass-textured satin web, localized and fabricated. **Refract Labs** pioneered **Splinter-Crush** armory — rifles and melee weapons with a mechanical crushing chamber. Load a Splinter, crush it inside the weapon, and the synthetic Seam violently bursts out and envelops the weapon as a temporary **Enhancement**.

A body or kit that runs Splinter chambers or shatter lines is **Splintered**. On the table that is the shared mutation card. Stays through Stitching.

### Brilliance

**House Kardia** smiths alone make Brilliance gear. It is powered through a contained Bright Core, or built to store and emit Bright energy. Stable. Pearl white, faintly radiant — calm contained light, not Tear neon. **Bright cells** are Brilliance shells holding cyan refined Bright energy. **Brilliance lets you handle Chroma safely:** Tears can touch the craft without the burn of bare skin on a Seam. Bloom still requires Chroma entering a body internally — Brilliance is external hazard protection when contact happens. Kardia do not deal in Tears or Chroma as a resource, and they refuse Chroma-fuel. Civic mornings and war frames share Brilliance craft. **Zenith Relays** distribute Bright energy as district substations. Containment units are Kardia Null-Flint harvesting hardware: pearl frames with golden Core-vault hearts — extract a Core intact instead of shattering it into Splinters.

### Chroma-fuel

Raw Chroma burned as fuel. Refract Labs designed the concept in the Resting Light. Kardia refuse it and ban it. Defected smiths and inventors carry the heresy to **the Aureate**, who weaponize it. **Chromatic fuel tanks** feed devastating, volatile arms. Sometimes a stolen Brilliance frame runs Chroma through the line anyway. Far less stable than Brilliance Core power. The only fuel of its kind on Aethora until now.

### Pure Bright and the Vessel

Proximity to the Core is the heaviest temporal alteration Aethora knows. It feels kin to Splintering: fleeting, extreme, a rewrite that will not stay. The only way to hold that scale of power and survive it is to absorb a Bright Core into a living **Vessel**. Nobody has survived that. Lumari still hunt that person. The final Vessel title has not settled.

## Who spends what

**Intruders** take Tears at weeping Seams, and Splinters when the spike is the plan. **House Kardia** forge Brilliance, cyan Bright cells, Zenith Relays, and containment — oppressed Brightsmiths; quantum-field Seam science. **Refract Labs** pioneered Splinter-Crush. **The Aureate** spend stolen Cores, Chroma-fuel, and Altered Humans. **Kho'Lan** wield raw Null-Flint only — indigenous apex-predator doctrine. **Lumari** drink Tears — divine will. **Penumbra** Voice Bloomed targets and cleanse Seams.

## What leaving costs

A Bright that collapses or loses its Core seeds young elsewhere. Chroma farmed outside still answers to Bright energy spent to open those Seams. The ledger is planetary.
