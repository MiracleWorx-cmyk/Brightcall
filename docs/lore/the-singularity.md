# The Bright

The Bright is the central anomaly on Aethora: one hyper-saturated sphere of glass-like light, slightly squished, roughly a kilometer across. Silent when it looks balanced. Blinding when you look straight. It feeds on Chroma. Under pressure it sings for living matter.

Matches of **Brightcall** happen inside it. From the **Eclipse** inward, the world is spliced — civic house against tundra against ancient rock — on Bone, Ash, and Greige ground. Ambient Bright energy across the bowl is generally survivable. Proximity to the Core is lethal.

Physics and board reading: [board glossary](pocket-and-core.md). Art: [style guide](../gdd/art-direction.md).

## Physical rules

**Bounded.** The Eclipse is the outer rim. It expands as the Core grows stronger.

**Umbras.** Darkest weak spots along the Eclipse. Safe entry. On the table: deployment and Stitching return. Deep Plum shadow.

**Seams.** Always a Bright-energy effect. Paper-thin satin curtains of shattered glass erupt like a net cast to catch prey, envelop a thing, and rewrite everything inside while they last. They always weep: Chroma (Magenta, Cyan, Searing Gold) oozes at the edges. They are fleeting. When a Seam ends, the original returns. Kho'Lan and Adumbra can cleanse them. Old omen: *"The seams are weeping."*

**Splinters.** Bright-powered Null-Flint shards. Inside a match they are a primary way crews cast Seams.

**Call of the Light.** Hypnotic pull toward the Bright Core. Living matter feeds unrest until the sphere is satisfied.

**Two energies.** Chroma is the liquid that weeps from Seam edges. Bright energy is Core emission and the force that causes Seams. Bright energy near the Core is lethal in close proximity.

**Mood is climate.** Glimmer / New-Light. Radiance / High Bright. Sature / harvest. Glare / the Blinding.

**Legend beats.** First Strike ended the first sphere and birthed Splinters. First Harvest contained a Core and ended the Traveling Light. Resting Light cities live on Brightforged power and the next seed.

**Death is Stitching.** Return to your Umbra. Splintered gear stays. Tempo worsens.

**Collapse ends this Bright.** No body outside if the sphere closes on you.

## Visual style

**Bright-punk Fantasy.** Bone, Ash, Greige world. Bruised Darkness shadows. Toxic neon Chroma on shattered Seam glass. Spliced luminous interior. Faction accents in the art guide. Umbras are Eclipse weak spots for entry and deployment.

## Dangers

| Danger | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Call of the Light | The song thickens near weeping Seams and the Core |
| Seam eruption | A net of shattered glass wraps and rewrites a thing for a time |
| Seam touch | Always dangerous. Can Bloom, burn, fuel, or kill |
| Disappearance | Collapse, or a first entry never Stitched |
| Stitching | Back to Umbra. Build intact. Tempo lost to someone else |
| Weeping Chroma | Loot and hazard. Bloom risk |
| Pure Bright | Core proximity. Fleeting godhood. No survivors of absorption |
| The next seed | A lost Core or collapsed Bright becomes someone else's New-Light |
