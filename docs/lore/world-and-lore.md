**Brightcall** is the game. **Aethora** is the world. **Chroma** is torn-reality energy. **Bright energy** is Core emission. Intruders say Tears. Refract Labs coined Chroma for that liquid. They do not work Bright energy.

If a card needs a fact that is not here, add the fact here first.

## Why they are in the arena

People enter a Bright through an Umbra. Someone wants a Core out, a Seam pinched, a Bloom survived, a Splinter tested, or another faction weaker at the rim. Call of the Light pulls. If they die first, the mission fails.

**Locked.** Stitching returns a patterned body to its Umbra. Build stays. Tempo worsens.

**Locked.** One Bright, from the inside. Eclipse at the rim. Seams are fleeting Bright-energy curtains that envelop and rewrite while active, always weep Magenta / Cyan / Searing Gold at the edges, then end and restore the original. Umbras as deployment. Bone / Ash / Greige ground. Spliced terrain. No Seam hex borders.

**Working.** Event deck: balance failing. Omen means hill, oddball, kill window, shove, harvest pressure toward the Blinding.

## Turning points

**The First Bright.** One known Bright. Kho'Lan divinity. First Survivor. Lumari form. Kardia begin to branch. First Strike ends that sphere.

**The Traveling Light.** Reseeding Brights. Roads and camps. First Harvest: Kardia contain a Bright Core with Null-Flint vacuum. Age ends.

**The Resting Light.** Fixed sites. Cities. Brightforge. Refract Labs splits, coins Chroma for Bright-Tears, and invents Chroma-fuel. Kardia ban it. The Aureate steal Cores, weaponize Chroma-fuel, and rule as a cutthroat wealth-nation. Kardia remain larger and happier under theft. Adumbra in the walls. Present.

## Loop

A lost Core or collapsed Bright seeds New-Light elsewhere. Chroma can be farmed outside once Bright energy tears wild Seams. Settled order still buys tomorrow's Glimmer.
