# World and lore

Design lock for Aethora — the filter a card should pass before it enters the set.

Narrative lives in the [timeline](timeline.md), [The Bright](the-singularity.md), [board glossary](pocket-and-core.md), [Chroma and Bright](lore_the_energy.md), and [factions](../factions/factions.md). Words live in [terminology](../terminology.md).

If a new request conflicts with the leading lore, ask before changing these pages.

**Brightcall** is the game. **Aethora** is the world. **Chroma** is torn-reality energy. **Bright energy** is Core emission. Intruders say Tears. Refract Labs coined Chroma for that liquid. They do not work Bright energy.

If a card needs a fact that is not here, add the fact here first.

## Why they are in the arena

People enter a Bright through an Umbra — the darkest weak spot on an Eclipse face. Someone wants a Core harvested or destroyed, a Seam pinched, a Bloom survived, a Splinter tested, or another faction beaten to the prize. Call of the Bright pulls — **the calling** — form unknown to the living. If they die first, the mission fails.

**Stitching** returns a patterned body to its Umbra in a match. When a Core is harvested or Null-Flint-destroyed, the Bright implodes and Stitches survivors to their Eclipse crossing.

One Bright, from the inside: cubic volume, vast monolithic maze, brighter toward the Core. Eclipse is the cube’s faces — a one-way mirror (bright/see-through outside; dark smoky inside). Umbras are dark doorways on a face — entry only. The Bright quiets on organic material and turns violent while consuming Harvesters. Consumption spends Bright energy as Seams that wrap living prey — **Enhancements**. Leave only by ending the Core (Null-Flint harvest or destroy). Seams burst Chroma at eruption and weep while active. External Chroma = damage; Bloom only if internal. Bone / Ash / Greige monoliths. No Seam hex borders.

The event deck is the fiction of balance failing. An omen becomes a hill, an oddball, a kill window, a shove, harvest pressure toward the Blinding.

## Turning points

**The First Light.** One known Bright. Kho'Lan divinity. First Survivor. Lumari form. Kardia begin to branch. First Strike ends that Bright.

**The Traveling Light.** Reseeding Brights. Roads and camps. First Harvest: Kardia contain a Bright Core with Null-Flint vacuum. Age ends.

**The Resting Light.** Fixed sites. Cities. Brilliance. Zenith Relays. Refract Labs splits, coins Chroma for Bright-Tears, and invents Chroma-fuel. Kardia ban it. The Aureate steal Cores, weaponize Chroma-fuel, Bloom foot soldiers under control, and rule as baron wealth. Kardia remain larger and happier under theft. Adumbra in the walls. Present.

## Loop

A lost Core or collapsed Bright seeds New-Light elsewhere. Chroma can be farmed outside once Bright energy tears wild Seams. Settled order still buys tomorrow’s Glimmer.

## Six answers

**Kho'Lan** keep the catch, read The Bright as a living predator, pinch Seams as digestion to leave alone. **Lumari** Bloom hard — drink Tears, read The Bright as divine will, hunt a Vessel. **Kardia** are the civic heart: Brilliance, Bright cells, Zenith Relays, containment craft, industrial reality-disruption science. **Refract Labs** coined Chroma and chase trials, Chroma-fuel, and scavenged Splinters. **The Aureate** run stolen Cores, Chroma-fuel, injected controlled Bloom infantry — barons stay out. **Adumbra** suppress or cure Bloom, cleanse Seams, and steer the exposed.

## Missions that fit

Hold a weeping Seam for a vault. Reach the Core and harvest with Null-Flint containment (Bright ends; Stitching). Destroy the Core with Null-Flint into Splinters. Pinch the Seam before Chroma leaves. Hijack a containment unit. Bloom and survive. Suppress another crew’s Bloom.
