# The Factions

Social order of the Resting Light on Aethora. Physics: [board glossary](pocket-and-core.md). Origins: [timeline](timeline.md). Names: [terminology](../terminology.md).

Cities stay lit because someone walks an Umbra and comes back heavier. The next Bright opens because they do. That bargain has landlords.

A Bright Core in **Kardia** custody is light, craft, and civic hope. A Bright Core in **Aureate** hands is a weapon, a market, and a leash. Kardia alone forge containment units and true Brightforged gear. The Aureate cannot. They steal finished Cores, hijack nearly full containment, and burn Chroma-fuel they did not invent the skill to tame. **Refract Labs** imagined that fuel. Kardia banned it. Defected smiths built it for the rich.

Two nations face each other. Kardia: more people, working economy, happier mornings, held back by theft. The Aureate: fewer people, extreme wealth beside extreme poverty, cutthroat supremacy, laborers held by the dream of infinite riches. **Adumbra** hold secrets between them. Refract Labs, **Kho'Lan**, and **Lumari** refuse both nations' terms.

## Kho'Lan

**Motif.** The held ground. Kho is the living world that does not weep. Lan is the promise to leave it whole.

**Origin.** Oldest temper on Aethora. First Bright reverence. They pronounce a catch between Kho and Lan, and the catch is the doctrine: Aethora and the Bright are not one word. Conservatives who refused the meddle when Kardia branched away. They pinch weeping Seams with ritual, Null-Flint, and other Aethorian materials, and guard against Core theft that would starve the song for a dock.

**Play.** Silence. Denial. Empty Discharge. Earthbound silhouette. White, red, and green.

## Lumari

**Motif.** Bloom zealots wearing faith. They take Tears into the body for power and hunt a **Vessel** — a person who can absorb a Bright Core and live. Nobody has. Final title open.

**Origin.** Fanatics of the First Bright. After the First Survivor — Bloomed, dying, strong, leaking Tears, wrapped in rags — they organized. Ritual drinking of leaked Tears killed most and tempered a few. Tolerance became hierarchy. Rags became tradition. Pure Bright absorption remains the unachieved end. Refract Labs later named that liquid Chroma. Lumari still say Tears.

**Play.** Health as fuel. Bloom lines. Fast adaptation. Cyan and magenta.

## Kardia

**Motif.** The civic heart. Larger nation. Brightforged craft. Containment skill. Working prosperity under Aureate pressure.

**Origin.** Branched from Kho'Lan after the First Survivor. Perfect life, controlled, in sync with the Bright. First Harvest belongs to them: Null-Flint vacuum around a Core. They house that heart so a city can beat. Resting Light cities are their geometry. They process Tears for districts and refuse to burn them. They alone build containment units and stable Brightforged weapons and civic gear — powered by Cores, or made to store and emit Bright energy.

**Society.** More people than the Aureate. Strong working economy. Happier. Not as rich. They survive and prosper, yet Aureate dealings steal Cores, skim wealth, and hold them back. They keep civic manners and etiquette under that pressure. Pride bruises easily; they still refuse to let ego write doctrine.

**Play.** Adapt. Finite stable Brightforged charge. Outmaneuver mass. Blue and silver hard-light.

## Refract Labs

**Motif.** The firm that renamed Bright-Tears. Science without the civic leash: Splinters, Bloom trials, Chroma-fuel, every use of the leak.

**Origin.** After the First Harvest, Kardia science split. Refract Labs coined Chroma for the liquid the street calls Tears. They wanted stable Bloom, perfected Splinters, human trials, and raw Chroma burned as fuel. Kardia banned that fuel. Refract Labs kept inventing. They work Chroma. They do not work the Bright, and they do not handle Bright energy. Morals optional.

**Play.** Spike and flee. Hot vaults. Splintered tails that break loud. Green and orange heat.

## The Aureate

**Motif.** Wealthiest power of the Resting Light: vicious, cutthroat, short on craft knowledge, long on opportunity.

**Origin.** Kardia who craved more. Successful Core theft made them a rival nation. They outgrew Kardia in wealth. They still lack the skill to build containment units, so they keep hijacking Kardia hardware. Defected smiths and inventors brought them Chroma-fuel: Resting Light heresy, banned by Kardia, devastating and volatile. Chromatic fuel tanks feed stolen Brightforged frames and purpose-built weapons. The fuel's volatility rewards ill intent.

**Society.** Few extremely rich. Many extremely poor laborers. Fewer people, less happiness. The poor stay for the illusion of infinite wealth.

**Play.** Hold the objective. Biggest tanks. Late hijack of containment. Mass. Chroma-fuel overclock. Death that breaches fuel lines. Purple, white, and gold.

## Adumbra

**Motif.** Secret order in every wall: pity for the amplified, hands on the Chroma-exposed.

**Origin.** Last into the Resting Light. Resent what Chroma and the Bright have done. Extreme of Kho'Lan refusal, with a rare art: suppress Bloom, cure Bloom, manipulate anyone already opened by Chroma. Few can do it. The right exposure is an invitation.

**Play.** Push, pull, steer. Cleanse Seams. Leverage. Never permanently steal a Splintered card. Stitching protects the build. Black, purple, and orange.
