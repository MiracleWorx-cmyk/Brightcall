# IV. The Factions

In the Resting Light, power on Oros answers to **The Bright** — a vast cube of light with a spherical Bright Core burning at its heart. More than one cube can stand across the world; Seed maps tell cities where the next rising can come. Crews find a dark Umbra on a face, walk into a peristaltic brutalist maze under fractured gravity, fight and harvest under Seam weather, and leave only when Null-Flint ends the Core. When that Bright dies, another must grow elsewhere. Cities keep their mornings at Seed intersections, and weapons keep their charge, on what those crews bring home — or on what is stolen from the bringers.

Six factions shape that struggle. Each is a motive and a habit, not a damage type.

**The Oru** are Indigenous Wardens who refused the industrial harvest. They wield Null-Flint, pinch Seams, and leave the predator to its seasons.

**Lumari** are a deeply devoted spiritual order. They drink Tears toward excess Bloom and hunt a Vessel who can absorb a Core and live.

**House Kardia** is a large city-nation and the former Bright-order superpower. Brightsmiths still forge Brilliance, Bright cells, Zenith Relays, and containment. The Aureate seized control. Kardia keep the craft under pressure and refuse Chroma-fuel.

**Refract Labs** split from Kardia after the First Harvest. They coined Chroma, pioneered Splinter-Crush, and treat the cube as a resource to weaponize.

**The Aureate** are former Kardia who took the Bright order by syndicate methods — bribery, theft, extortion, hijacked containment. They burn Chroma-fuel and send controlled-Bloom infantry into the arena in heavier metal.

**Penumbra** work the shadows of that struggle. They resent the Bright as unnatural, look down on dependence, and manipulate Bloomed cells and bodies to pressure politics — method still open.

The chapters that follow take each faction in turn: creed, craft, fear, and the first draft faces that will walk the cube.
