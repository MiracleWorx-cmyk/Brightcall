# VII. House Kardia

A large Orosian city-nation. Former Bright-order superpower. Brightsmith pride and superior craft remain even after the Aureate seized control.

They branched from Oru ground after the First Survivor. The First Harvest belongs to them. They alone build Null-Flint containment. They refuse Chroma-fuel and do not deal in Tears as a resource. Industrial science of Seams is still unfinished — labs speak of a quantum model of The Bright that does not yet exist as settled doctrine.

**Material.** **Brilliance** — extremely refined pearl-white metal, carefully cast to fit the wearer. Rare and expensive now: once abundant, then scarce as the Aureate stole Bright Cores. The cast keeps a Harvester nimble, handles Chroma safely, powers weapons, and conducts Bright energy.

**Energy.** **Bright cells** — standardized interchangeable spheres or cylinders in Brilliance shells, holding bright-white refined Bright energy. Cyan is a Chroma color, not a cell color. **Zenith Relays** distribute that charge through districts.

**Vibe.** Fake-stoic, proud, elegant. High-fashion trenchcoats over sparse cast Brilliance — Vesper’s measure is one full arm and shoulder, plus one glove on the other hand. Never a sealed mech. Civic-modern magitech under ash: crests polished, manners held, charge finite.

**Want:** Core custody returned; mornings that still open under their craft.  
**Fear:** extinction, or winning by becoming the Aureate.

**At the table:** adaptable. Finite Bright cell charge. Outmaneuver mass. Blue and silver hard-light on pearl frames. They do not own plant-and-hold — the Aureate do.
