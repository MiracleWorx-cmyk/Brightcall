# The Bright — board glossary

Physics for the interior of The Bright, and the words the board needs. History: [timeline](timeline.md). Energies: [Chroma and Bright](lore_the_energy.md). Words: [terminology](../terminology.md).

## The words

**The Bright** is a vast cube of hyper-saturated light. Exact edge length is open. It feeds on Chroma from Seams only — it does not eject Chroma. It hungers for living matter. It quiets on organic material; when it consumes Harvesters it grows more violent. Consumption takes time and spends Bright energy as **Seams** cast onto the prey — that volatility rewrites Harvesters (augments on the table). Matches happen inside it. Brighter ground means stronger Bright energy.

**The Eclipse** is the outer boundary — the six faces of the cube. Outside: bright, see-through into a Seam-fractured interior. Inside: dark smoky barrier hiding Aethora.

**Call of the Bright** is the siren toward the Bright Core. Street may say **the calling**. Manifestation unknown. The game title **Brightcall** echoes that pull.

A **Seam** is a paper-thin shattered satin curtain caused by Bright energy. It erupts like a cast net, envelops and rewrites while active, and always weeps Chroma at the edges. Fleeting as a curtain — terrain and objects restore when it ends. Brief residue may linger and hinder navigation without permanently rewriting matter. **Consumption Seams** on living Harvesters are the Bright’s appetite; a partial living rewrite that sticks mid-match is an **augment**.

An **Umbra** is the darkest weak spot on an Eclipse face — a dark doorway in a wall of light: entry, deployment, mid-match Stitching return. Not an exit. Once inside, only Null-Flint Core harvest or destroy ends the Bright.

**Chroma** — also Tears, Bright-Tears on the street — is the liquid that oozes from Seam edges only. Magenta, Cyan, Searing Gold. Harvestable widely.

**Bright energy** is Core emission, and also the force that causes Seams. Stronger where the light is brighter. Lethal in close Core proximity. Looks bright white for now.

A **scratch** is when a Seam almost opens — Tears may weep along the cut before a full curtain lands.

The **Bright Core** is a reverse black hole of light. It emits Bright energy. Seams crowd it and weep Chroma. Interior look unknown. **Harvest** means taking the Core — faction motives differ. Null-Flint harvesting equipment extracts it into containment instead of shattering it into Splinters. **Destroy** throws Null-Flint into the Core and shatters it into Splinters. Either ending implodes the Bright.

**Null-Flint** is Aethorian vacuum crystal. It implodes on ignition. Kardia use it for containment; Kho'Lan use it in Seam craft.

**Splinters** are Bright-powered Null-Flint shards. They carry Bright energy and are a primary way to cast Seams.

**Bloom** is Chroma in living cells. Touching a Seam can cause it.

**Splintered** means altered with a Splinter — the match mutation card.

**Brilliance** is Kardia craft powered by a contained Bright Core, or built to store and emit Bright energy. Stable. Pearl white, faintly radiant. The metal lets a body handle Chroma safely. **Bright cells** are Brilliance packs holding bright-white charge. **Zenith Relays** are Kardia energy substations.

**Chroma-fuel** is raw Chroma burned as fuel. Refract Labs imagined it. Kardia ban it. The Aureate weaponize it. Volatile.

A **containment unit** is Kardia Null-Flint vacuum hardware that holds a Bright Core. Golden Core-vault look. Proper name still open.

A **chromatic fuel tank** holds Chroma for Chroma-fueled weapons. Pressurized canister look. Proper name still open.

A **Vessel** is a person who can absorb a Bright Core and live. Nobody has. Final title still open.

**Stitching** is death’s return to the Umbra in a match (build stays). Collapse of a Bright by harvest or Null-Flint destroy Stitches survivors back to their Eclipse crossing.

**Discharge** is extracting a containment unit, or pinching a Seam (pinch name still open). Both pay victory points.

## What the board is

One match is one Bright, played from the inside. A monolithic maze of gates and concrete — the Bright’s defense — fills the cube with chokepoints. Spliced hexes and Seam tokens share the floor. Umbras sit on dark inner Eclipse faces as deployment. The Core hangs at the dead center, overexposed. Edge hexes near the inner Eclipse read darker; center hexes bleach under Core light. Proximity to the Core is lethal.

Seams erupt as predatory nets — upright curtain tokens on or over hexes. They are not printed hex borders. While a Seam lasts, whatever it wraps is rewritten and Chroma weeps at the edges. When the Seam ends, the original returns. Residue may briefly block or confuse a route.

## When the seams weep

*“The seams are weeping.”*

Old Aethoran omen. Every Seam weeps. Chroma is in the air. Call of the Bright thickens — the calling is loud. Harvest weather. The Blinding may be near.

Pressurized Magenta, Electric Cyan, and Searing Gold ooze at the curtain edges. Occupy, cross, or touch: hurt and pay. In lore, physical touch is always dangerous and can Bloom. On the table, exposure can mean fuel, Bloom, damage, or death.

Street says Tears / Bright-Tears. Intruders Splinter gear with shards when they have them — and cast Seams with that Bright power.

Corporate says Chroma — the lab name Refract Labs gave Bright-Tears. Null-Flint vacuum into a containment unit. Refract Labs run hot and Splinter everything. The Aureate burn Chroma-fuel.

Bloom is the body taking Tears. Lumari path. Seams are a common source.

Near the nucleus, Bright energy dominates. Extract under vacuum into a Kardia containment unit for Brilliance custody. Pure exposure is fleeting temporal rewrite — lethal scale, not soft transcendence. Absorbing the Core into a Vessel is the Lumari end goal. Nobody has lived it.

Outside the cube, Bright energy from a held Core can force Seams so Chroma weeps in the wild.

Being shoved off an active Seam dumps an unfinished fill. Banked points stay.

## Discharge

**Harvest:** secure a Bright Core with Null-Flint harvesting equipment / containment. The Core is taken intact instead of shattered into Splinters. That Bright ends in violent implosion. Survivors Stitch to their Eclipse crossing. A young Bright seeds elsewhere. Umbras are entry only.

**Destroy:** Null-Flint into the Core. Splinters. Implosion. Same collapse Stitching for survivors not erased by the strike.

**Pinch:** Kho'Lan (and Adumbra cleanses) shut a Seam before its natural end. The pinch name is still open. No vault from that act.

Harvest or pinch Discharge pays victory points with kills on the same track.

## What the board sees

A zone or hill is often an active Seam curtain — fill and exposure work. An oddball is a containment unit in transit. Push and pull shove figures into or off a Seam. Kills bloom near live Seams or objectives. **Splintered** is the shared mutation offer from Splinters — draw three, keep one on those rounds — and often the cast that throws a Seam. **Stitching** drops you, returns you to Umbra, and keeps Splintered cards, levels, and points.

Round 15 finishes a build. The match ends at 15 victory points.

## Factions, one physics

**Kardia** spend Brilliance, Bright cells, Zenith Relays, and containment units. They refuse Chroma-fuel and do not deal in Tears as a resource. **Refract Labs** coined Chroma; they run Chroma trials, Chroma-fuel theory, and scavenged Splinters as spent Bright-charge tools — they do not contain Cores, forge Brilliance, or run Zenith Relays. **Kho'Lan** pinch and cleanse Seams with Null-Flint, field procedure, and other Aethorian materials. **The Aureate** hijack containment, burn Chroma-fuel weapons, run controlled Bloom on foot soldiers, and keep baron leaders out of The Bright. **Lumari** take Tears into the body, interact with weeping Chroma, and Bloom toward a Vessel. **Adumbra** suppress Bloom, cleanse Seams, and steer the exposed.
