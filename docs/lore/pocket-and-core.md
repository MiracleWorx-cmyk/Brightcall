## What the board is

One match is one Bright, played from the inside. Spliced hexes share a floor because The Bright rewrote unlike realities into one bowl: civic house against tundra against ancient rock. Umbras sit along the Eclipse as deployment. The Core hangs at the dead center. Ambient Bright energy across the bowl is generally survivable. Proximity to the Core is lethal.

Seams erupt as predatory nets — upright curtain tokens on or over hexes. They are not printed hex borders. While a Seam lasts, whatever it wraps is rewritten and Chroma weeps at the edges. When the Seam ends, the original returns.

## When the seams weep

*"The seams are weeping."*

Old Aethoran omen. Every Seam weeps. Chroma is in the air. Call of the Light thickens. Harvest weather. The Blinding may be near.

Pressurized Magenta, Electric Cyan, and Searing Gold ooze at the curtain edges. Occupy, cross, or touch: hurt and pay. In lore, physical touch is always dangerous and can Bloom. On the table, exposure can mean fuel, Bloom, damage, or death.

**Street.** Tears / Bright-Tears. Intruders Splinter gear with shards when they have them — and cast Seams with that Bright power.

**Corporate.** Chroma — the lab name Refract Labs gave Bright-Tears. Null-Flint vacuum into a containment unit. Refract Labs run hot and Splinter everything. The Aureate burn Chroma-fuel.

**Bloom.** Body takes Tears. Lumari path. Seams are a common source.

**Core work.** Near the nucleus, Bright energy dominates. Extract under vacuum into a Kardia containment unit for Brightforged custody. Pure exposure is fleeting temporal rewrite. Absorbing the Core into a Vessel is the Lumari myth. Nobody has lived it.

**Outside farm.** Bright energy from a held Core can force Seams beyond the bowl so Chroma weeps in the wild.

Being shoved off an active Seam dumps an unfinished fill. Banked points stay.

## Discharge

**Extract.** Walk a containment unit out through an Umbra. This sphere quiets. A young Bright seeds elsewhere.

**Pinch.** Kho'Lan (and Adumbra cleanses) shut a Seam before its natural end. Pinch name open. No vault from that act.

Either Discharge pays victory points with kills on the same track.

| --- | --- |
| Zone / hill | Active Seam curtain. Fill / exposure work. |
| Oddball | Containment unit in transit. |
