# VI. Lumari

A deeply devoted spiritual order. Bloom extremists who take Tears into the body for power and hunt a **Vessel** — a person who can absorb a Bright Core and live. Nobody has. The final title is still open.

They are lineages of the First Light. After the First Survivor — Bloomed, dying, strong, leaking Tears, wrapped in rags — they organized. Lineage trials drinking leaked Tears killed most and tempered a few. Tolerance became hierarchy. Rags became practical kit for bodies that keep rewriting. Their Bloom is **excessive**: extreme mutation for assassination and suicide missions.

**In their telling.** The Bright is a conscious divine entity. Seams, Chroma, Enhancements, and arena brutality are deliberate acts and communication from a god. Bottling that power into Brilliance custody is blasphemy dressed as morning light.

**Want:** embodiment of Core-scale rewrite.  
**Fear:** science that cages the god in a vault.

**At the table:** health as fuel, Bloom lines, fast adaptation, lineage hierarchy. Cyan and magenta.

**Open design:** teleport through the cracks from which Seams erupt — in progress, not locked.

**Roster (working)**

1. **The Bloomed Zealot** — Rags. Consumes Tears at weeping Seams for permanent mid-match Blooms.
2. **The Lineage Martyr** — Lesser Lumari. Takes environmental cost or spends actions to feed Tears into the Zealot’s Bloom. Built for lethal one-way runs.
