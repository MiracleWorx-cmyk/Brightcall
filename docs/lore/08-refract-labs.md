# VIII. Refract Labs

Scientific pragmatists and weapon-manufacturers. The firm that renamed Bright-Tears. Speech shortens the name to Refract.

They split from Kardia after the First Harvest — researchers who wanted every use-case Kardia refused to bless. Morals optional. Results loud.

**Perspective.** The Bright is a volatile resource meant to be exploited, studied, and weaponized — not a god, not sacred nature.

**Technology.** Pioneers of **Splinter-Crush**. Specialize in synthesizing and controlling localized Seams for advanced Bright-punk weaponry. Chroma research and Chroma-fuel theory. They do not contain Cores, forge Brilliance, or run Zenith Relays.

**Want:** maximum thresholds; controlled Seam weaponry.  
**Fear:** a tame Chroma, or civic use that leaves the burn and the crush forbidden.

**At the table:** spike and flee, Splinter-Crush Enhancements, overclock, honest self-damage. Green and orange heat.

**Roster (working)**

1. **The Test-Subject** — Heavily Splintered. Crush or shatter own Splinters for extreme synthetic Enhancement lines, then stand exposed.
2. **The Harvester** — Null-Flint opportunist. Smashing harvests for fresh Splinters; stays out until a containment unit is worth taking for lab data.
