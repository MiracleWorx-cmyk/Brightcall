# Terminology

Words drift faster than rules. This page is the list. Every page is English. A proper name does not go on a card, a standee, or a new page until it is written here.

If a prettier sentence disagrees with this page, this page wins.

This page is the lorebook Appendix. Narrative chapters live in [`docs/lore/`](lore/README.md). If a new request conflicts with this page or those chapters, ask before changing either.

Rules and components use the word written here. Fiction may speak in rumors; rumors are not second components. Retired words stay retired in narrative. Narrative states what things are — the correction voice belongs only on this page.

## The game and the world

The game is **Brightcall**. The world is **Oros**. The anomaly is **The Bright**. **Call of the Bright**, **the calling**, and **Brightcall** name the same multilayered pull toward the Core. Torn-reality energy is **Chroma** in the lab and **Bright-Tears** / **Tears** on the street. Core radiation is **Bright energy** — not the same substance as Chroma. Safe entry is an **Umbra** (plural Umbras). The outer boundary is **the Eclipse**.

## Two energies

**Chroma** is reality torn by the force of the Bright. Seams release it. Street mouths call it Bright-Tears or Tears. The Bright consumes Chroma to feed itself. It can be harvested where Seams erupt and weep, including Seams forced open outside a Bright using Bright energy from a Core. Chroma is **rareish and dangerous** — valued, feared, never casual décor. **External** skin contact with Chroma is a painful damaging hazard — it does not Bloom. A **Bloom** happens only when Chroma enters the body **internally** (eaten, drunk, or injected).

**Bright energy** is emitted near the Bright Core, inside a Bright. It can be harvested only inside a Bright, close to the Core. It is the heavy radiance of the nucleus — usually spoken of as “the Core” or the Bright itself. Its look is **bright white** / off-white shine — never cyan (cyan is Chroma). Refined **Bright cells** hold that same white charge.

**Streams** are a **visual phenomenon** of Bright energy — calm white stream at rest, stronger wind when flowing, faint smoky cloud on impact — always extremely subtle, never Chroma jet fog. A stream does **not** mean the charge is leaking away. Unenforced, it is only a sign that a Bright source is near (the same quiet white presence fills The Bright’s cube). When **enforced or empowered**, cities can leverage those streams to move power through the air (civic Bright transmission). When **concentrated**, that same force is what thins and cuts the fabric of reality — the pressure behind Seams and scratches.

Pure exposure is extreme temporal alteration: fleeting, overwhelming. It is the heaviest alteration short of taking a Core into the body. The only way to hold and survive that scale of power is to absorb a Bright Core. Nobody has survived absorbing a Bright Core. That survival is the Lumari end goal.

## Peoples

**Humans** are the most common race of Oros — the default city crowds of Kardia and the Resting Light nations. Pale to varied human skin; ordinary stature beside Oru mass. Most Brightsmiths, barons, laborers, and Harvesters who are not called out as another people read as human. **Valen** is human.

**Oru** are a people of Oros: the oldest indigenous race — very large stature, black skin with a faint stone glimmer, bodies durable like carved stone. Exterior reads mass and hardness. Interior feeling is still loud. Few remain in the industrial cities; an Oru in Kardia stands out among human crowds. Leading visual: `site/public/art/hero-harvester.png` (Vesper). **The Oru** as a faction name means the Indigenous Wardens who refused harvest industry — not every Oru by blood.

## Nomenclature

**The Bright** is the central anomaly on Oros — a vast **cube** of hyper-saturated light that feeds on Chroma and hungers for living matter. Exact edge length is open. The cubic exterior matches the brutalist monolithic interior and makes entry easy to read: you approach a face of light. Inside it is very bright. Brighter ground means more potent Bright energy; brightness climbs toward the Core. The living maze moves in peristaltic waves that guide organic matter inward. Fractured gravity favors parkour leaps between shifting blocks. Torn Kardia and Aureate structures appear as poor reflections of outside reality fused into the cage. One Bright per match. Across Oros, more than one cube can stand at once — each is still **The Bright** to whoever faces it. Faith may call every cube one reincarnated Bright; industry maps many instances of one relocating anomaly. The Bright **quiets** when it receives **organic material**. When Harvesters enter, it tries to consume them too; **during consumption it grows more violent**. That consumption takes time. The Bright energy spent on the attempt erupts as **Seams** that wrap living prey — that is the volatility inside the Bright, and that living wrap is an **Enhancement**.

A **Seed** is a Bright spawning zone on Oros — ground where a Bright can rise when another ends. When a Bright becomes unsustainable and dies (Core harvested or Null-Flint-destroyed), physics demands that the anomaly exist and grow elsewhere. That relocation is the loop that keeps The Bright in harmony with Oros. Resting Light scientists mapped Seed patterns; civilization sits at strategic intersections between Seeds. Street may still say New-Light for a young rising.

**Call of the Bright**, **the calling**, and the game title **Brightcall** name the same pull — multilayered wording for one hunger that draws living matter toward the Core. Inside a Bright, that pull reads as peristaltic maze motion and fractured gravity guiding organics inward. How the call sounds or feels to a living mind is still unknown — nobody has heard it and lived to tell.

A **Seam** is a paper-thin transparent satin sheet — razor-thin cloth or web — that shoots out of nowhere when Bright energy overwhelms reality. Texture reads as fractured broken glass. Always caused by Bright energy. It violently wraps an object or Harvester and rewrites what it wraps while it lasts. At the exact moment of eruption / injection, Magenta, Cyan, and Searing Gold **Chroma** burst violently from the cracks and linger at the origin in the air. While the Seam lasts, those Tears also **weep** together through the cracks and along the edges — continuous weep plus the eruption burst. The sheet itself is clear fractured glass-satin, not a monochrome cyan or magenta curtain. Fleeting as a curtain — when a Seam on terrain or an object ends, the original returns. Unstable Seam residue / afterglow can linger briefly and make navigation hard; that residue is not a permanent rewrite scar. Old saying: “The seams are weeping.” On the table: an upright curtain token with a timer. No permanent Altered scar token after a curtain ends.

When a Seam wraps a **Harvester** or a **weapon**, the temporary rewrite is an **Enhancement**. Appetite Seams wrap living prey during consumption. Synthetic Seams from crushed Splinters wrap Bright-punk armory. Volatile and dangerous. On the table, appetite wraps draft as Enhancements; Splinter-Crush wraps live on the Splintered path. Full consume by the Bright still kills. Enhancement is distinct from **Bloom** (permanent internal Chroma mutation).

A **scratch** (reality scratch) is the moment a Seam almost opens — Bright vibration or force thinning reality until Tears weep along the cut without a full curtain token yet. Scratches can deepen into Seams. On the table: residue or chromatic damage options; not automatically a lasting Seam token.

**The Eclipse** is the outer boundary of The Bright — the six faces of the cube. It expands as the Core grows stronger. One-way: from outside a face is bright and you can see into a fractured, Seam-thick interior; from inside the same face is a dark, smoky barrier that hides Oros. On the table: the edge of the arena.

An **Umbra** is the darkest weak spot on an Eclipse face — a dark doorway cut into a wall of light. That is **entry**. On the table: deployment and mid-match Stitching return. Once inside, crews do not walk out. The only ways the Bright ends — and survivors leave — are rare **Null-Flint** acts against the Core.

The **Bright Core** is the nucleus — a perfectly smooth blinding-white **sphere** of liquid light; a reverse black hole of light. Square maze geometry yields to that round Core. It emits enormous Bright energy. It does not eject Chroma. An abundance of Seams crowds the Core, and those Seams ooze Chroma. What the **inside** of a Core looks like is unknown. **Harvest** means taking a Bright Core. Factions want that Core for different ends. On the table: extraction / collapse objective at center.

**Null-Flint** is Orosian vacuum crystal — hammer and ignition that implodes. Two Core endings use it:

- **Destroy:** throw Null-Flint into the Core. Vacuum. The Core shatters into **Splinters**. The Bright ends in violent implosion.
- **Harvest / extract:** Null-Flint **harvesting equipment** (Kardia containment craft) pulls the Core into custody instead of shattering it into Splinters. The Bright still ends in violent implosion. Survivors Stitch to their Eclipse crossing.

**The Oru** rely on **Null-Flint** tech — Seam-closing craft and analog vacuum combat. They reject Bright cells, Chroma-fuel, and Splinters. Null-Flint is rare.

**Splinters** are Bright-powered Null-Flint shards. They form when Null-Flint violently meets a Bright Core — smashed against it in a harvest, or born when a Core shatters under Null-Flint (First Strike / destroy). Each shard holds dormant, highly unstable Bright energy. Intentionally crushing or destroying a Splinter triggers a **synthetic Seam**: a localized fabricated tear that bursts out as the same glass-textured satin web. Material for the Splintered card; synthetic Enhancement fuel.

**Bloom** is a permanent severe biological mutation. It happens only when Chroma enters the body **internally** — eaten, drunk, or injected. External skin contact burns and damages; it does not Bloom. Lumari drink Tears toward excess Bloom. The Aureate inject foot soldiers — controlled-Bloom infantry — with raw Chroma for controlled Bloom so those bodies can withstand crude Chroma-fuel heat and pain.

**Enhancement** is the temporary tactical rewrite when a Seam wraps a Harvester or a weapon. New combat power, changed fighting style or armament. Sources: the Bright’s consumption Seams on living prey, or **synthetic** Seams from crushed Splinters. On the table: the shared Enhancement draft deck covers appetite wraps; Splinter-Crush weapon wraps live on the Splintered path. Draft tiers (Weak / Moderate / Ultimate) and Path A (global / structural) versus Path B (slot / trigger) are **table language only** — not lore nouns. In the world, wraps vary a great deal.

**Splinter-Crush** is Bright-punk armory tech pioneered by **Refract Labs**. A rifle or melee weapon carries a mechanical crushing chamber. Load a Splinter, crush it inside the weapon, and a synthetic Seam violently bursts out and envelops the weapon — a temporary weapon **Enhancement**.

**Splintered** means altered with Splinter craft — crushing chambers, loaded shards, or body-mounted shatter lines. On the table: the shared Splintered mutation card. Stays through Stitching. Synthetic Enhancement path, distinct from appetite **Enhancement** drafts.

**Brilliance** is Kardia metalcraft: extremely refined pearl-white metal, carefully cast to fit the wearer — organic, living curves rather than hard machine lines. Rare and expensive — scarcer since Aureate Core theft. Powered by a contained Bright Core or built to store and emit Bright energy. Keeps a body nimble, handles Chroma safely, powers weapons. It is a **solid-state resonant conductor** of Bright — internal micro-fluting carries charge without cables, plugs, or hoses. It conducts only a portion of what a cell holds — lossy enough that **cube Bright cells** must stay **easy to swap**. The shell carries a very light blue gleam; the charge shining through stays **bright white / off-white**. Blue gleam is metal sheen, not cyan Chroma.

**Resonance** is a Kardia craft family: ultrasonic Brilliance tools that hum at high frequency and can thin reality into a **scratch**. True Glaive-class Resonance designs are forged in a **Bright Core–powered smithy** — Core-fed forge infrastructure. The finished weapon runs on **Bright cells**, not on a Core seated in the blade. Vesper’s **Resonance Glaive** is the peak of that line (Valen’s design). Aureate Chroma substitutes may borrow Resonance *shape* and still belong to the family in street talk, but they burn **Chroma-fuel** and work differently — Valen’s working **Resonance Dagger** (final name TBD) is that corrupt cousin: no Bright cell, no Core-smithy purity.

A **Bright cell** is a standardized interchangeable **cube** of Brilliance that holds Bright energy — about Rubik’s-cube scale (~7.5 cm / 3 in), dense, with circular cutouts over a glass core and pin connectors for mount receivers. Working pattern mark: **Eminence-pattern (VE-3)**. The charge reads as **bright white** light inside the Brilliance shell. Finite. Harvesters **swap** charged cells into a mount; empties are discarded (not field-recharged). Civic residents may need only a couple across a lifetime; a hungry tool like a Resonance Glaive burns a Tithe of two or three in a single harvest run. Cyan is a Chroma/Tear color — not the cell. Concept: `docs/gdd/references/bright-cell-schematic.png`, `docs/gdd/references/vesper-full-bright-cell.png`.

A **Zenith Relay** is Kardia civic tech: an energy substation that distributes Bright energy through a district. Appearance still open. Stable civic infrastructure.

**Chroma-fuel** / chromatic fuel is raw Chroma burned as fuel. Refract Labs concept. Kardia ban it — Chroma use carries hard social stigma there (dirty, unnatural, hazard to bystanders). The Aureate weaponize it with defectors and exiles (**Chroma-Smiths** such as Valen). Volatile. Devastating. Resting Light heresy. Aureate overclock / death-burst craft. Exhaust reads about **60% Magenta**, **35% Cyan**, **15% Gold**.

A **chromatic fuel tank** holds raw Chroma for Chroma-fueled weapons. Proper model name still open. Aureate resource / hazard.

A **containment unit** is Null-Flint vacuum hardware that holds a Bright Core for transport. Only Kardia can make these. Proper name still open. Oddball / extract piece.

A **Vessel** is a person who can absorb a Bright Core and live. Nobody has. Final title still open. Lumari endgame.

**Stitching** is how the Bright returns a patterned body. In a match, death returns you to your Umbra with the build intact — that is not leaving the anomaly; the Bright still holds the match. When a Bright ends by Core harvest or Null-Flint destruction, a violent implosion follows: the Bright is gone. Bodies not annihilated in the Null-Flint strike itself die and are Stitched back to when they crossed the Eclipse.

**Discharge** is harvesting a Core into a containment unit, or pinching a Seam (pinch name still open). Victory points.

Match mutations are Splintered, done with Splinters.

Vessel is a person. Containment unit holds a Bright Core. Chromatic fuel tanks hold Chroma for weapons. Those three do not swap.

Brilliance, Chroma-fuel, and Splinter-Crush are different industrial applications. Brilliance / Bright cells are Kardia Core craft: stable electricity and power. Chroma-fuel is Aureate brute mechanical force from raw Chroma burn. **Refract Labs** specialize in synthesizing and controlling localized Seams for tactical Bright-punk weaponry — Splinter-Crush chambers, Chroma research, Chroma-fuel theory. They do not contain Cores, forge Brilliance, or run Zenith Relays.

Street says Tears, or Bright-Tears. That liquid is the Chroma oozing from Seam edges only. The Bright does not eject Chroma. Refract Labs coined Chroma. The lab word spread. Intruders / Harvesters are street crews, not a seventh faction.

Every Seam is caused by Bright energy — the Bright’s force inside the anomaly, or Bright energy released when a Splinter is crushed. Players cast synthetic Seams through Splinter-Crush. Whether the event deck ever casts Seams is still open.

A Seam is one transparent shattered satin sheet. It erupts from reality, rewrites everything it wraps while active, bursts Chroma at eruption, and weeps mixed Magenta / Cyan / Gold through its cracks while active. A wrap on a Harvester or a weapon is an Enhancement. It is fleeting. When the Seam ends, the original attributes return. There is no permanent Altered scar.

The Oru and Penumbra can cleanse / pinch Seams. Lumari may take weeping Chroma into the body. External contact with Chroma or a Seam is always dangerous as damage hazard. Bloom requires internal entry. On the table, exposure can mean fuel, damage, or death depending on the card; Bloom lines require an internal-path card.

Tear / Chroma colors on the board are only Pressurized Magenta, Electric Cyan, and Searing Gold. Seam glass may look clear or fractured; that is the curtain, not a Tear. Bright energy’s white light is not a Tear color.

## Mood of the Bright

Formal speech says Glimmer, Radiance, Sature, the Glare. Street says New-Light, High Bright, harvesting season (street fall-name still open), the Blinding.

## The eras

**The First Light** — one known Bright. Culture, Call of the Bright, Oru divinity. First Survivor. Lumari coalesce. Kardia begin to branch. First Strike.

**The Traveling Light** — peoples follow reseeding Brights before Seed patterns are known. First Harvest: Kardia contain a Bright Core with a Null-Flint device.

**The Resting Light** — Seed patterns mapped; cities at Seed intersections; fixed Bright sites. Refract Labs splits. The Aureate steal Cores, outgrow Kardia in wealth, rule as a cutthroat nation. Chroma-fuel heresy. Penumbra in the walls. The present.

First Strike belongs to the First Light era. First Harvest belongs to the Traveling Light age and ends it. The Resting Light age begins when contained Cores make settled order possible.

## First Survivor, First Strike, First Harvest

The First Survivor walked out of the Bright heavily Bloomed: dying, extraordinarily strong, Tears leaking from the Blooms. Fanatics treated the Survivor as living proof a body could hold Bright rewrite. Rags worn to cover mutations that clothing would not fit become Lumari tradition. Lineage trials with the Survivor’s leaked Tears often killed; sometimes they left small alterations. Lineages that lived gained tolerance. Tolerance is Lumari hierarchy.

Rath lost family to the Call of the Bright. First Strike: Null-Flint into the Bright Core. Vacuum. Splinters born. That Bright ends. Young Brights seed elsewhere.

First Harvest: Kardia contain a Bright Core with a Null-Flint-powered device that implodes into vacuum instead of exploding. Brilliance civic power begins. Traveling Light ends. Resting Light begins.

## Stitching

In a match, **Stitching** returns you to your Umbra / deployment edge. Splintered cards, levels, stats, and victory points stay. Health, shields, marks, and temporary words clear.

When a Core is harvested or destroyed, the Bright implodes and ends. Survivors of that collapse who were not erased by the Null-Flint strike itself are Stitched to their Eclipse crossing.

## Factions

**the Oru** — Indigenous Wardens. Working faction name for the people who refused industrial harvest. **Null-Flint**-reliant; anti-harvest; natural harmony. Reject Bright cells, Chroma-fuel, and Splinters. The Bright is living predator and season-maker; leave it alone. Ideological opposite of Penumbra. Race and faction share the name **Oru**; city Oru (like Vesper) are not automatically wardens.



**Lumari** — deeply devoted spiritual order. Bloom lineages after the First Survivor. They take Tears into the body and hunt a Vessel. Excess Bloom for assassination and suicide missions. In-universe view: The Bright is a conscious divine entity; Seams, Chroma, and arena brutality are deliberate acts and communication from a god.

**House Kardia** — large city-nation; former Bright-order superpower. Brightsmiths still forge Brilliance, bright-white Bright cells, Zenith Relays, containment, and Resonance craft in Bright Core–powered smithies. Aureate (ex-Kardia) seized control through syndicate methods. Refuse Chroma-fuel. Industrial Seam science / quantum talk still in progress. Fake-stoic elegance; varied civic coats and fashion over sparse cast Brilliance. Vesper’s trenchcoat is her personal silhouette — not house uniform.



**Refract Labs** — the table shortens this to Refract. Scientific and weapon-manufacturing pragmatists. Split from Kardia after the First Harvest. Coined Chroma for Bright-Tears. Invented Chroma-fuel theory. Pioneered **Splinter-Crush**. They treat The Bright as a volatile resource to exploit, study, and weaponize — not a god, not a sacred predator. Specialize in synthesizing and controlling localized Seams for advanced Bright-punk armory. They do not contain Cores, forge Brilliance, or run Zenith Relays. Share the industrial scientific worldview of Seams as reality disruption.

**the Aureate** — “the” belongs to them. Former Kardia; barons and crime lords who seized the Bright order by bribery, theft, extortion, and hijacked containment. Burn Chroma-fuel. Heavier metal frames than Kardia. Barons stay out. Foot soldiers are **controlled-Bloom infantry** with oversized Chroma canisters and long hoses. **Valen** — human Chroma-Smith exile and elite Architect; refined ex-jewel-smith; calculated; wealth as penance; heart with Kardia; does not strike first; no Brilliance after exile; white high-collar formal coat-vest (not a trench); horological Chroma prosthesis with flush ampules; temple Resonance ear (Bright-punk sense, not HUD); Resonance-inspired break-point dagger; same 60 / 35 / 15 exhaust law. Share unfinished industrial Seam science with Kardia and Refract.



**Penumbra** — shadow order. The Bright is unnatural; they resent it and look down on Bright/Chroma dependence and greed. They manipulate Bloomed cells and bodies (method still open) to pressure global politics, including Aureate Bloomed infantry. Can suppress or cure Bloom and cleanse Seams. Ideological opposite of the Oru.



Lumari, Kardia, and Penumbra stand without “the.” **The Oru** and **the Aureate** keep “the.” Refract Labs is the company name.

Faction accents: Kardia blue and silver hard-light on pearl Brilliance; the Aureate purple, white, and gold; Refract Labs green and orange heat; Lumari cyan and magenta; the Oru white, red, and green; Penumbra black, purple, and orange. Containment units read as golden Core-vaults on pearl frames; chromatic fuel tanks read as pressurized canisters.

## Aesthetic

**Bright-punk** (genre label): between fantasy and sci-fi — a modern-gloomy steampunk cousin. Extreme high contrast. Sin City read: Bone / Ash / Greige and bruised dark as the ground, then saturated Magenta / Cyan / Gold Chroma accents. Zero-digital. Civic-modern magitech + anomaly physics. Bright energy and Bright cells: bright white. UI: Rich Cream, Structural Gold, cyan→magenta gradients. North-star plate: `site/public/art/hero-harvester.png`.



Seams are fleeting transparent shattered satin sheets caused by Bright energy. They erupt from reality, envelop, rewrite while active, burst Chroma at eruption, and weep Magenta / Cyan / Gold together through the cracks — never as separate monochrome colored curtains. Harvester or weapon wrap = Enhancement. Splinter-Crush casts synthetic Seams. Arcane / Altered neighborhood as reference. Call of the Bright / the calling / Brightcall as one multilayered pull. No blood, fleshy gore, or wound imagery for Seams or marketing stills. The arena is inside The Bright — a cube of light with a peristaltic brutalist maze, fractured gravity, and a spherical white Core. Hard tech limit: **no** digital technology, computers, screens, LEDs, internet, or modern firearms. Power is Bright energy (refined/stable, bright white) or Chroma (raw/volatile neon). Splinter-Crush chambers are mechanical anomaly armory. Brilliance is rare cast-to-fit metal under cloth — Kardia/Vesper body budget: one full arm and shoulder, plus one glove on the other hand.
