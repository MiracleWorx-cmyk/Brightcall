# Terminology

Words drift faster than rules. This page is the list. Every page is English. A proper name does not go on a card, a standee, or a new page until it is written here.

If a prettier sentence disagrees with this page, this page wins.

Aethora lore as of this page is the source of truth. If a new request conflicts with this page or with `docs/lore/`, ask before changing either.

Rules and components use the word written here. Fiction may speak in rumors; rumors are not second components. Retired words stay retired in narrative. Narrative states what things are — the correction voice belongs only on this page.

## The game and the world

The game is **Brightcall**. The world is **Aethora**. The anomaly is **The Bright**. The siren is **Call of the Bright** — street may say **the calling**; the game title echoes that pull. Torn-reality energy is **Chroma** in the lab and **Bright-Tears** / **Tears** on the street. Core radiation is **Bright energy** — not the same substance as Chroma. Safe entry is an **Umbra** (plural Umbras). The outer boundary is **the Eclipse**.

## Two energies

**Chroma** is reality torn by the force of the Bright. Seams weep it. It can be harvested anywhere Seams can be made to weep, including Seams forced open outside a Bright using Bright energy from a Core. The Bright consumes Chroma to feed itself. Street mouths call it Bright-Tears or Tears. It changes cells; a successful bind is a **Bloom**.

**Bright energy** is emitted near the Bright Core, inside a Bright. It can be harvested only inside a Bright, close to the Core. It is the heavy radiance of the nucleus — usually spoken of as “the Core” or the Bright itself. Pure exposure is extreme temporal alteration: fleeting, overwhelming. It is the heaviest alteration short of taking a Core into the body. The only way to hold and survive that scale of power is to absorb a Bright Core. Nobody has survived absorbing a Bright Core. That survival is the Lumari end goal.

## Nomenclature

**The Bright** is the central anomaly on Aethora — hyper-saturated glass-like light that feeds on Chroma and sings for living matter. One Bright per match.

**Call of the Bright** pulls living matter toward the Core — pressure near weeping Seams and the Core. Street speech may say **the calling**. **Brightcall** is the game; the title carries that same hunger into the table.

A **Seam** is a paper-thin satin curtain of shattered glass. Always caused by Bright energy. It erupts like a cast net, envelops and rewrites a thing while it lasts, and always weeps: Chroma oozes at the edges. Fleeting — when it ends, the original returns. Old saying: “The seams are weeping.” On the table: an upright curtain token with a timer. No permanent scar.

**The Eclipse** is the outer boundary of The Bright. It expands as the Core grows stronger. On the table: the edge of the arena.

An **Umbra** is the darkest weak spot along the Eclipse — safe entry. On the table: the deployment edge.

The **Bright Core** is the nucleus and source of Bright energy. Ambient exposure across the bowl is generally survivable; proximity is lethal. On the table: extraction objective / center of the bowl.

**Null-Flint** is Aethorian vacuum crystal — hammer and ignition that implodes. Used in Kardia containment and in Kho'Lan Seam-closing craft.

**Splinters** are Bright-powered Null-Flint shards (First Strike origin). They carry Bright energy and are a primary way crews cast Seams in a match. Material for the Splintered card; Seam cast fuel.

**Bloom** is Chroma bound into living tissue. Lumari call the liquid Tears.

**Splintered** means altered with a Splinter — the shared mutation card. It stays through Stitching.

**Brightforged** is made by Kardia smiths: powered by a contained Bright Core, or built to store and emit Bright energy. Stable. Civic and war gear. Kardia reliability fantasy.

**Chroma-fuel** / chromatic fuel is raw Chroma burned as fuel. Refract Labs concept. Kardia ban it. The Aureate weaponize it with defected smiths. Volatile. Devastating. Resting Light heresy. Aureate overclock / death-burst fantasy.

A **chromatic fuel tank** holds raw Chroma for Chroma-fueled weapons. Proper model name still open. Aureate resource / hazard.

A **containment unit** is Null-Flint vacuum hardware that holds a Bright Core for transport. Only Kardia can make these. Proper name still open. Oddball / extract piece.

A **Vessel** is a person who can absorb a Bright Core and live. Nobody has. Final title still open. Lumari endgame fantasy.

**Stitching** is how death returns: the Bright stitches a patterned body back to its Umbra. It wants living matter. Respawn to Umbra. Build stays.

**Discharge** is extracting a containment unit, or pinching a Seam (pinch name still open). Victory points.

Match mutations are Splintered, done with Splinters.

Vessel is a person. Containment unit holds a Bright Core. Chromatic fuel tanks hold Chroma for weapons. Those three do not swap.

Brightforged and Chroma-fuel are different applications. Brightforged is Kardia Core craft: stable. Chroma-fuel is banned raw burn: volatile. Refract Labs work Chroma. They do not work the Bright or Bright energy.

Street says Tears, or Bright-Tears. That liquid is the Chroma oozing from Seam edges. Refract Labs coined Chroma. The lab word spread. Intruders are street crews, not a seventh faction.

Every Seam is caused by Bright energy — the Bright’s force inside the anomaly, or Bright energy carried in tools such as Splinters. Players can cast Seams (especially through Splinters). Whether the event deck ever casts Seams is still open.

A Seam is an enveloping shattered-glass curtain. It erupts like a predatory net, rewrites everything it wraps while active, and always weeps. It is fleeting. When the Seam ends, the original attributes return. There is no permanent Altered scar.

Kho'Lan and Adumbra can cleanse / pinch Seams. Lumari may interact with the Chroma that weeps from them. Physically touching a Seam is always dangerous in lore and can cause a Bloom. On the table, exposure can mean fuel, Bloom, damage, or death depending on the card.

Tear / Chroma colors on the board are only Pressurized Magenta, Electric Cyan, and Searing Gold. Seam glass may look clear or fractured; that is the curtain, not a Tear.

## Mood of the Bright

Formal speech says Glimmer, Radiance, Sature, the Glare. Street says New-Light, High Bright, harvesting season (street fall-name still open), the Blinding.

## The eras

**The First Light** — one known Bright. Culture, Call of the Bright, Kho'Lan divinity. First Survivor. Lumari coalesce. Kardia begin to branch. First Strike.

**The Traveling Light** — peoples follow reseeding Brights. First Harvest: Kardia contain a Bright Core with a Null-Flint device.

**The Resting Light** — fixed sites. Refract Labs splits. The Aureate steal Cores, outgrow Kardia in wealth, rule as a cutthroat nation. Chroma-fuel heresy. Adumbra in the walls. The present.

First Strike belongs to the First Light era. First Harvest belongs to the Traveling Light age and ends it. The Resting Light age begins when contained Cores make settled order possible.

## First Survivor, First Strike, First Harvest

The First Survivor walked out of the Bright heavily Bloomed: dying, extraordinarily strong, Tears leaking from the Blooms. Fanatics worshipped the Survivor as the human shape of the deity in the Bright. Rags worn to cover mutations that clothing would not fit become Lumari tradition. Ritual drinking of the Survivor’s leaked Tears often killed; sometimes it left small alterations. Lineages that lived gained tolerance. Tolerance is Lumari hierarchy.

Rath lost family to the Call of the Bright. First Strike: Null-Flint into the Bright Core. Vacuum. Splinters born. That Bright ends. Young Brights seed elsewhere.

First Harvest: Kardia contain a Bright Core with a Null-Flint-powered device that implodes into vacuum instead of exploding. Brightforged civic power begins. Traveling Light ends. Resting Light begins.

## Stitching

The return is **Stitching**. You appear at your Umbra / deployment edge. Splintered cards, levels, stats, and victory points stay. Health, shields, marks, and temporary words clear.

## Factions

**Kho'Lan** — say KO-lan, with a catch between the halves. Oldest temper. Kho is the living world that does not weep. Lan is the promise to leave it whole. The stop keeps Aethora and the Bright from being one word. Pinch Seams with ritual, Null-Flint, and other Aethorian materials. Balance.

**Lumari** — Bloom lineages after the First Survivor. They take Tears into the body and hunt a Vessel.

**Kardia** — branched from Kho'Lan. The civic heart. They house the Bright Core, Brightforge, and refuse Chroma-fuel.

**Refract Labs** — the table shortens this to Refract. Split from Kardia after the First Harvest. Coined Chroma for Bright-Tears. Invented Chroma-fuel. They do not work the Bright or Bright energy.

**the Aureate** — “the” belongs to them; the box may read Aureate. Left Kardia. Wealthiest power. Steal Cores. Hijack Kardia containment. Burn Chroma-fuel they cannot forge.

**Adumbra** — secret order. Suppress or cure Bloom. Steer the Chroma-exposed.

Kho'Lan, Lumari, Kardia, and Adumbra stand without “the.” Refract Labs is the company name. Kho'Lan keeps the apostrophe; a spelling that drops the catch is an outsider smoothing the name.

Faction accents: Kardia blue and silver hard-light; the Aureate purple, white, and gold; Refract Labs green and orange heat; Lumari cyan and magenta; Kho'Lan white, red, and green; Adumbra black, purple, and orange. Containment units read as golden Core-vaults; chromatic fuel tanks read as pressurized canisters.

## Aesthetic

**Bright-punk Fantasy.** Physical world: Bone, Ash, Greige. Shadows: Bruised Darkness (Deep Plum, Industrial Olive, Charcoal). Chroma: Pressurized Magenta, Electric Cyan, Searing Gold. UI: Rich Cream, Structural Gold, cyan→magenta gradients.

Seams are fleeting paper-thin shattered satin curtains caused by Bright energy. They erupt, envelop, rewrite while active, always weep Magenta / Cyan / Gold Chroma at the edges, then end and restore the original. Arcane / Altered neighborhood as reference. Call of the Bright as psychological pull. No blood, fleshy gore, or wound metaphors for the Bright or Seams. The arena is inside The Bright.
