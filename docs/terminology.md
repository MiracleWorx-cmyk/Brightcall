# Terminology

Words drift faster than rules. This page is the list. Every page is English. A proper name does not go on a card, a standee, or a new page until it is written here.

If a prettier sentence disagrees with this page, this page wins.

**Leading lock.** Aethora lore as of this page is the source of truth. If a new request conflicts with this page or with `docs/lore/`, ask before changing either.

## How a sentence should pick

- Rules and components use the word in the table.
- Fiction may speak in rumors. Rumors are not second components.
- Retired words stay retired in narrative.
- Narrative states what things are. Correction voice belongs only here.

## Two energies

| | Chroma | Bright energy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Origin | Reality torn by the force of the Bright. Seams weep it. | Emitted near the Bright Core, inside a Bright. |
| Harvest | Anywhere Seams can be made to weep, including Seams forced open outside a Bright using Bright energy from a Core. | Only inside a Bright, close to the Core. |
| Role | The Bright consumes Chroma to feed itself. | The heavy radiance of the nucleus. |
| Street | Bright-Tears, Tears | usually just "the Core" / Bright |
| Alteration | Changes cells. Successful bind = **Bloom**. | Pure exposure = extreme temporal alteration. Fleeting, overwhelming. |

**Locked.** Chroma and Bright energy are not the same substance. Chroma is the leak of torn reality. Bright energy is what the Core puts out at close range.

**Locked.** Pure Bright exposure is the heaviest alteration short of taking a Core into the body. It behaves like Splintering in that it is fleeting and extreme. The only way to hold and survive that scale of power is to absorb a Bright Core. Nobody has survived absorbing a Bright Core. That survival is the Lumari end goal.

**Locked.** There is no Weld. Match mutations are Splintered, done with Splinters.

**Locked.** Vessel is a person. Containment unit holds a Bright Core. Chromatic fuel tanks hold Chroma for weapons. Do not swap those three.

**Locked.** Brightforged and Chroma-fuel are different applications. Brightforged is Kardia Core craft: stable. Chroma-fuel is banned raw burn: volatile. Refract Labs work Chroma. They do not work the Bright or Bright energy.

**Locked.** Street says Tears, or Bright-Tears. That liquid is the Chroma oozing from Seam edges. Refract Labs coined Chroma. The lab word spread. Intruders are street crews, not a seventh faction.

**Locked.** Every Seam is caused by Bright energy — the Bright's force inside the anomaly, or Bright energy carried in tools such as Splinters. Players can cast Seams (especially through Splinters). Event-deck Seam casting is not locked yet.

**Locked.** A Seam is an enveloping shattered-glass curtain, not a ground crack and not a hex border. It erupts like a predatory net, rewrites everything it wraps while active, and always weeps: Chroma oozes at the edges. It is fleeting. When the Seam ends, the original attributes return. There is no permanent Altered scar.

**Locked.** Kho'Lan and Adumbra can cleanse / pinch Seams. Lumari may interact with the Chroma that weeps from them. Physically touching a Seam is always dangerous in lore and can cause a Bloom. On the table, exposure can mean fuel, Bloom, damage, or death depending on the card.

**Locked.** Tear / Chroma colors on the board are only Pressurized Magenta, Electric Cyan, and Searing Gold. There is no White Tears color. Seam glass may look clear or fractured; that is the curtain, not a Tear.

| The First Bright | One known Bright. Culture, Call of the Light, Kho'Lan divinity. First Survivor. Lumari coalesce. Kardia begin to branch. First Strike. |
| The Traveling Light | Peoples follow reseeding Brights. First Harvest: Kardia contain a Bright Core with a Null-Flint device. |
| The Resting Light | Fixed sites. Refract Labs splits. The Aureate steal Cores, outgrow Kardia in wealth, rule as a cutthroat nation. Chroma-fuel heresy. Adumbra in the walls. The present. |

**Locked.** First Strike belongs to the First Bright era. First Harvest belongs to the Traveling Light age and ends it. The Resting Light age begins when contained Cores make settled order possible.

## First Survivor, First Strike, First Harvest

**Locked.** The First Survivor walked out of the Bright heavily Bloomed: dying, extraordinarily strong, Tears leaking from the Blooms. Fanatics worshipped the Survivor as the human shape of the deity in the Bright. Rags worn to cover mutations that clothing would not fit become Lumari tradition. Ritual drinking of the Survivor's leaked Tears often killed; sometimes it left small alterations. Lineages that lived gained tolerance. Tolerance is Lumari hierarchy.

**Locked.** Rath lost family to the Call of the Light. First Strike: Null-Flint into the Bright Core. Vacuum. Splinters born. That Bright ends. Young Brights seed elsewhere.

**Locked.** First Harvest: Kardia contain a Bright Core with a Null-Flint-powered device that implodes into vacuum instead of exploding. Brightforged civic power begins. Traveling Light ends. Resting Light begins.

**Locked.** "The" belongs to the Aureate. Kho'Lan, Lumari, Kardia, and Adumbra stand without it. Refract Labs is the company name.

**Locked.** Kho'Lan keeps the apostrophe. A spelling that drops the catch is an outsider smoothing the name.

**Locked.** Faction accents: Kardia blue and silver hard-light; the Aureate purple, white, and gold; Refract Labs green and orange heat; Lumari cyan and magenta; Kho'Lan white, red, and green; Adumbra black, purple, and orange. Containment units read as golden Core-vaults; chromatic fuel tanks read as pressurized canisters.

## Aesthetic

**Bright-punk Fantasy.** Physical world: Bone, Ash, Greige. Shadows: Bruised Darkness (Deep Plum, Industrial Olive, Charcoal). Chroma: Pressurized Magenta, Electric Cyan, Searing Gold. UI: Rich Cream, Structural Gold, cyan→magenta gradients.

Seams are fleeting paper-thin shattered satin curtains caused by Bright energy. They erupt, envelop, rewrite while active, always weep Magenta / Cyan / Gold Chroma at the edges, then end and restore the original. Arcane / Altered neighborhood as reference. Call of the Light as psychological pull. No blood, fleshy gore, or wound metaphors for the Bright or Seams. The arena is inside The Bright, not a factory.
