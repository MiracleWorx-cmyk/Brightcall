# Terminology

Words drift faster than rules. This page is the list. Every page is English. A proper name does not go on a card, a standee, or a new page until it is written here.

If a prettier sentence disagrees with this page, this page wins.

Aethora lore as of this page is the source of truth. If a new request conflicts with this page or with `docs/lore/`, ask before changing either.

Rules and components use the word written here. Fiction may speak in rumors; rumors are not second components. Retired words stay retired in narrative. Narrative states what things are — the correction voice belongs only on this page.

## The game and the world

The game is **Brightcall**. The world is **Aethora**. The anomaly is **The Bright**. The siren is **Call of the Bright** — street may say **the calling**; the game title echoes that pull. Torn-reality energy is **Chroma** in the lab and **Bright-Tears** / **Tears** on the street. Core radiation is **Bright energy** — not the same substance as Chroma. Safe entry is an **Umbra** (plural Umbras). The outer boundary is **the Eclipse**.

## Two energies

**Chroma** is reality torn by the force of the Bright. Seams weep it. It can be harvested anywhere Seams can be made to weep, including Seams forced open outside a Bright using Bright energy from a Core. The Bright consumes Chroma to feed itself. Street mouths call it Bright-Tears or Tears. It changes cells; a successful bind is a **Bloom**.

**Bright energy** is emitted near the Bright Core, inside a Bright. It can be harvested only inside a Bright, close to the Core. It is the heavy radiance of the nucleus — usually spoken of as “the Core” or the Bright itself. For now its look is **bright white** light; finer hue language is still open. Pure exposure is extreme temporal alteration: fleeting, overwhelming. It is the heaviest alteration short of taking a Core into the body. The only way to hold and survive that scale of power is to absorb a Bright Core. Nobody has survived absorbing a Bright Core. That survival is the Lumari end goal.

## Peoples

**Oru** are a people of Aethora: very large stature, black skin with a faint stone glimmer, bodies durable like carved stone. Exterior reads mass and hardness. Interior feeling is still loud. Leading visual for an Oru Harvester: `site/public/art/hero-harvester.png` (Vesper).

## Nomenclature

**The Bright** is the central anomaly on Aethora — a vast **cube** of hyper-saturated light that feeds on Chroma and hungers for living matter. Exact edge length is open. The cubic exterior matches the brutalist monolithic interior and makes entry easy to read: you approach a face of light. Inside it is very bright. Brighter ground means more potent Bright energy; brightness climbs toward the Core. One Bright per match. The Bright **quiets** when it receives **organic material**. When Harvesters enter, it tries to consume them too; **during consumption it grows more violent**. That consumption takes time. The Bright energy spent on the attempt erupts as **Seams** cast onto and around the prey — that is the volatility inside the Bright, and that is how Harvesters get altered.

**Call of the Bright** pulls living matter toward the Core. Street speech may say **the calling**. **Brightcall** is the game; the title carries that same hunger into the table. How the call manifests is unknown — nobody has heard it and lived to tell.

A **Seam** is a paper-thin transparent satin sheet that erupts from reality — like looking through a shattered window draped over something. Always caused by Bright energy. It envelops and rewrites a thing while it lasts. Magenta, Cyan, and Searing Gold **Chroma** drip and ooze **together** through the cracks of that sheet and along its edges. The sheet itself is clear fractured glass-satin, not a monochrome cyan or magenta curtain. Fleeting as a curtain — when a Seam on terrain or an object ends, the original returns. Unstable Seam residue / afterglow can linger briefly and make navigation hard; that residue is not a permanent rewrite scar. Old saying: “The seams are weeping.” On the table: an upright curtain token with a timer. No permanent Altered scar token after a curtain ends.

**Consumption Seams** are Seams the Bright casts onto living Harvesters while it tries to eat them. The rewrite is volatile and dangerous: living tissue, fighting pattern, even what Resting Light labs call DNA. It takes time. A partial bind that sticks through the match is how **augments** work on the table — new powers and a changed fighting style, still risky. Full consume is death or worse. This is distinct from **Bloom** (Chroma bound into tissue) and from **Splintered** (engineered Splinter mutation).

A **scratch** (reality scratch) is the moment a Seam almost opens — Bright vibration or force thinning reality until Tears weep along the cut without a full curtain token yet. Scratches can deepen into Seams. On the table: residue or chromatic damage options; not automatically a lasting Seam token.

**The Eclipse** is the outer boundary of The Bright — the six faces of the cube. It expands as the Core grows stronger. One-way: from outside a face is bright and you can see into a fractured, Seam-thick interior; from inside the same face is a dark, smoky barrier that hides Aethora. On the table: the edge of the arena.

An **Umbra** is the darkest weak spot on an Eclipse face — a dark doorway cut into a wall of light. That is **entry**. On the table: deployment and mid-match Stitching return. Once inside, crews do not walk out. The only ways the Bright ends — and survivors leave — are rare **Null-Flint** acts against the Core.

The **Bright Core** is the nucleus — a reverse black hole of light. It emits enormous Bright energy. It does not eject Chroma. An abundance of Seams crowds the Core, and those Seams ooze Chroma. What the inside of a Core looks like is unknown. **Harvest** means taking a Bright Core. Factions want that Core for different ends. On the table: extraction / collapse objective at center.

**Null-Flint** is Aethorian vacuum crystal — hammer and ignition that implodes. Two Core endings use it:

- **Destroy:** throw Null-Flint into the Core. Vacuum. The Core shatters into **Splinters**. The Bright ends in violent implosion.
- **Harvest / extract:** Null-Flint **harvesting equipment** (Kardia containment craft) pulls the Core into custody instead of shattering it into Splinters. The Bright still ends in violent implosion. Survivors Stitch to their Eclipse crossing.

Null-Flint is also used in Kho'Lan Seam-closing craft. It is rare.

**Splinters** are Bright-powered Null-Flint shards (First Strike origin). They carry Bright energy and are a primary way crews cast Seams in a match. Material for the Splintered card; Seam cast fuel.

**Bloom** is Chroma bound into living tissue. Lumari call the liquid Tears.

**Splintered** means altered with a Splinter — the shared mutation card. Engineered Bright-charge rewrite, often used to cast Seams on purpose. It stays through Stitching. Separate from consumption **augments**, which are the Bright’s appetite rewriting the Harvester through Seams.

**Brilliance** is Kardia metalcraft: machined pearl-white, faintly radiant, powered by a contained Bright Core or built to store and emit Bright energy. Stable. Civic and war gear. **Brilliance lets a body handle Chroma safely** — Tears can touch the craft without the raw Bloom/burn of bare contact. Blue and silver hard-light accents sit on that shell. Kardia reliability craft.

A **Bright cell** is a standardized interchangeable sphere or cylinder of Brilliance that holds Bright energy. The charge reads as bright white light inside the Brilliance shell. Finite. Reloadable. Field and civic power packs. Not Tear neon.

A **Zenith Relay** is Kardia civic tech: an energy substation that distributes Bright energy through a district. Appearance still open. Stable civic infrastructure.

**Chroma-fuel** / chromatic fuel is raw Chroma burned as fuel. Refract Labs concept. Kardia ban it. The Aureate weaponize it with defected smiths. Volatile. Devastating. Resting Light heresy. Aureate overclock / death-burst craft.

A **chromatic fuel tank** holds raw Chroma for Chroma-fueled weapons. Proper model name still open. Aureate resource / hazard.

A **containment unit** is Null-Flint vacuum hardware that holds a Bright Core for transport. Only Kardia can make these. Proper name still open. Oddball / extract piece.

A **Vessel** is a person who can absorb a Bright Core and live. Nobody has. Final title still open. Lumari endgame.

**Stitching** is how the Bright returns a patterned body. In a match, death returns you to your Umbra with the build intact — that is not leaving the anomaly; the Bright still holds the match. When a Bright ends by Core harvest or Null-Flint destruction, a violent implosion follows: the Bright is gone. Bodies not annihilated in the Null-Flint strike itself die and are Stitched back to when they crossed the Eclipse.

**Discharge** is harvesting a Core into a containment unit, or pinching a Seam (pinch name still open). Victory points.

Match mutations are Splintered, done with Splinters.

Vessel is a person. Containment unit holds a Bright Core. Chromatic fuel tanks hold Chroma for weapons. Those three do not swap.

Brilliance and Chroma-fuel are different applications. Brilliance is Kardia Core craft: stable, pearl white, faintly radiant, Bright cells of white charge, safe to handle Chroma through. Chroma-fuel is banned raw burn: volatile, dirty, neon-pressurized. Refract Labs work Chroma and scavenged Splinters. They do not contain Cores, forge Brilliance, or run Zenith Relays.

Street says Tears, or Bright-Tears. That liquid is the Chroma oozing from Seam edges only. The Bright does not eject Chroma. Refract Labs coined Chroma. The lab word spread. Intruders / Harvesters are street crews, not a seventh faction.

Every Seam is caused by Bright energy — the Bright’s force inside the anomaly, or Bright energy carried in tools such as Splinters. Players can cast Seams (especially through Splinters). Whether the event deck ever casts Seams is still open.

A Seam is one transparent shattered satin sheet. It erupts from reality, rewrites everything it wraps while active, and always weeps mixed Magenta / Cyan / Gold Chroma through its cracks. It is fleeting. When the Seam ends, the original attributes return. There is no permanent Altered scar.

Kho'Lan and Adumbra can cleanse / pinch Seams. Lumari may interact with the Chroma that weeps from them. Physically touching a Seam is always dangerous in lore and can cause a Bloom. On the table, exposure can mean fuel, Bloom, damage, or death depending on the card.

Tear / Chroma colors on the board are only Pressurized Magenta, Electric Cyan, and Searing Gold. Seam glass may look clear or fractured; that is the curtain, not a Tear. Bright energy’s white light is not a Tear color.

## Mood of the Bright

Formal speech says Glimmer, Radiance, Sature, the Glare. Street says New-Light, High Bright, harvesting season (street fall-name still open), the Blinding.

## The eras

**The First Light** — one known Bright. Culture, Call of the Bright, Kho'Lan divinity. First Survivor. Lumari coalesce. Kardia begin to branch. First Strike.

**The Traveling Light** — peoples follow reseeding Brights. First Harvest: Kardia contain a Bright Core with a Null-Flint device.

**The Resting Light** — fixed sites. Refract Labs splits. The Aureate steal Cores, outgrow Kardia in wealth, rule as a cutthroat nation. Chroma-fuel heresy. Adumbra in the walls. The present.

First Strike belongs to the First Light era. First Harvest belongs to the Traveling Light age and ends it. The Resting Light age begins when contained Cores make settled order possible.

## First Survivor, First Strike, First Harvest

The First Survivor walked out of the Bright heavily Bloomed: dying, extraordinarily strong, Tears leaking from the Blooms. Fanatics treated the Survivor as living proof a body could hold Bright rewrite. Rags worn to cover mutations that clothing would not fit become Lumari tradition. Lineage trials with the Survivor’s leaked Tears often killed; sometimes they left small alterations. Lineages that lived gained tolerance. Tolerance is Lumari hierarchy.

Rath lost family to the Call of the Bright. First Strike: Null-Flint into the Bright Core. Vacuum. Splinters born. That Bright ends. Young Brights seed elsewhere.

First Harvest: Kardia contain a Bright Core with a Null-Flint-powered device that implodes into vacuum instead of exploding. Brilliance civic power begins. Traveling Light ends. Resting Light begins.

## Stitching

In a match, **Stitching** returns you to your Umbra / deployment edge. Splintered cards, levels, stats, and victory points stay. Health, shields, marks, and temporary words clear.

When a Core is harvested or destroyed, the Bright implodes and ends. Survivors of that collapse who were not erased by the Null-Flint strike itself are Stitched to their Eclipse crossing.

## Factions

**Kho'Lan** — say KO-lan, with a catch between the halves. Oldest temper. Kho is the living world that does not weep. Lan is the promise to leave it whole. The stop keeps Aethora and the Bright from being one word. Pinch Seams with Null-Flint, field procedure, and other Aethorian materials. Balance.

**Lumari** — Bloom lineages after the First Survivor. They take Tears into the body and hunt a Vessel. Their Bloom is excessive: extreme mutation for assassination and suicide missions.

**Kardia** — branched from Kho'Lan. The civic heart. They house the Bright Core, forge Brilliance, run Zenith Relays, and refuse Chroma-fuel. They do not deal in Tears or Chroma as a resource. Brilliance lets their tools touch Chroma safely when contact happens; seeking Tears is not Kardia doctrine.

**Refract Labs** — the table shortens this to Refract. Split from Kardia after the First Harvest. Coined Chroma for Bright-Tears. Invented Chroma-fuel. Work scavenged Splinters. They do not contain Cores, forge Brilliance, or run Zenith Relays.

**the Aureate** — “the” belongs to them; the box may read Aureate. Left Kardia. Wealthiest power. Steal Cores. Hijack Kardia containment. Burn Chroma-fuel they cannot forge. Baron-class leaders rarely enter The Bright themselves. Foot soldiers receive **controlled Bloom** — measured Chroma exposure so bodies can survive raw fuel lines — distinct from Lumari excess Bloom.

**Adumbra** — secret order. Suppress or cure Bloom. Steer the Chroma-exposed.

Kho'Lan, Lumari, Kardia, and Adumbra stand without “the.” Refract Labs is the company name. Kho'Lan keeps the apostrophe; a spelling that drops the catch is an outsider smoothing the name.

Faction accents: Kardia blue and silver hard-light on pearl Brilliance; the Aureate purple, white, and gold; Refract Labs green and orange heat; Lumari cyan and magenta; Kho'Lan white, red, and green; Adumbra black, purple, and orange. Containment units read as golden Core-vaults on pearl frames; chromatic fuel tanks read as pressurized canisters.

## Aesthetic

**Bright-punk Fantasy** (genre label): civic-modern magitech + anomaly physics — not medieval soft fantasy. Physical world: Bone, Ash, Greige. Shadows: Bruised Darkness (Deep Plum, Industrial Olive, Charcoal). Chroma: Pressurized Magenta, Electric Cyan, Searing Gold. Bright energy: bright white. UI: Rich Cream, Structural Gold, cyan→magenta gradients.

Seams are fleeting transparent shattered satin sheets caused by Bright energy. They erupt from reality, envelop, rewrite while active, and weep Magenta / Cyan / Gold Chroma together through the cracks — never as separate monochrome colored curtains. Arcane / Altered neighborhood as reference. Call of the Bright as psychological pull. No blood, fleshy gore, or wound metaphors for the Bright or Seams. The arena is inside The Bright — a cube of light with a monolithic maze interior. Hard tech limit: no computers, screens, internet, or modern firearms; guns and lasers are infant tech.
