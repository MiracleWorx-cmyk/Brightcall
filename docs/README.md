# Brightcall

**Brightcall** is the game (one word). **Aethora** is the world.

A fast hex brawler played inside **The Bright**. The Bright tries to consume Harvesters — that appetite casts **Seams** that wrap them mid-match as **Enhancements**. A shared **Splintered** deck is the engineered mutation path. Kills and objectives score. First to 15 victory points.

Every page is English. The nouns live in [terminology](terminology.md). The look lives in [art direction](gdd/art-direction.md).

## What the world holds

Two energies share Aethora: **Bright energy**, which causes Seams and pours from the Core (stronger where brighter), and **Chroma** / Bright-Tears — Magenta, Cyan, and Searing Gold oozing at Seam edges only. The Bright does not eject Chroma.

**Seams** are fleeting transparent shattered satin sheets that erupt from reality. Looking through one is like a shattered window. At eruption Chroma bursts from the cracks; while active Magenta, Cyan, and Gold weep together at the edges. A Harvester wrap is an **Enhancement**. Terrain restores when the curtain ends. Brief residue may hinder routes. They are not hex borders. They leave no permanent rewrite scars.

**Splinters** are Bright-powered Null-Flint — a primary way crews cast Seams in a match. Players can cast Seams. Whether the event deck ever casts them is still open.

Kho'Lan and Adumbra cleanse and pinch Seams (the pinch name is still open). Lumari may work weeping Chroma. External Chroma contact is damage hazard; Bloom only if Chroma enters the body internally.

The Bright is a vast **cube** of light. The **Eclipse** is its six faces (bright/see-in from outside; dark smoky from inside). **Umbras** are dark doorways on a face — entry only. Once inside, leave only by ending the Core: Null-Flint **harvest** (extract into containment) or Null-Flint **destroy** (shatter into Splinters). The Bright quiets on organic material and turns violent while consuming Harvesters. Inside is a vast monolithic maze that defends its Core.

Applications: **Bloom**, **Splintered**, **Brilliance** (pearl white, faintly radiant; safe Chroma handling; Bright cells of bright-white charge), **Chroma-fuel**.

Factions: Kho'Lan, Lumari, Kardia, Refract Labs, the Aureate, Adumbra.

Aesthetic: **Bright-punk Fantasy** (civic-modern magitech + anomaly physics) — Bone / Ash / Greige, Bruised Darkness, toxic neon Chroma.

Eras: First Light → Traveling Light → Resting Light. Siren: **Call of the Bright** (street: **the calling**; manifestation unknown). **Stitching** returns match-death to Umbra, and collapse survivors to their Eclipse crossing. Core: reverse black hole of light; harvest or Null-Flint destroy ends the Bright.
